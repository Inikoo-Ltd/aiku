# Production

## Artisans belong to work areas; a department's artisans are those of the area where it is made
Work areas (work_areas, HELP-4463: Bath Bombs Area, Oils Area...) are where artisans physically work; the Production Manager (org-supervisor) adds, renames, deletes them and links product departments on the Artisans dashboard. A department is made in one area (artefact_departments.work_area_id); artisans join areas through artisan_assignments (artisanable_type WorkArea). Artisans are never assigned to an ArtefactDepartment directly (removed 3 Nov 2026). A department's artisans = the artisans of its area: pickers list them first (GetArtisanDepartments::departmentFirst / department_ids on options). When nobody is chosen (and the artefact has no artisan of its own), the default maker is the least busy of them: PickDepartmentArtisans (clocked in today first, then on the tablet in the last week, then fewest open job orders, then name; never someone with neither). Never pick the first artisan of a list, or every card defaults to whoever was added first. The board marks these picks with maker_is_auto. Performance and Payroll show and filter by area (GetArtisanWorkAreas).

## Step targets have dates: write them through SetArtefactStepTarget
HELP-4461 (3 Nov 2026): artefact_step_targets is the source of truth for a recipe step's target per hour; artefacts_manufacture_tasks.standard_rate only mirrors the target in force now. A session takes the target in force when it started (ManufactureTaskSession::stepStandardRate -> ArtefactStepTarget::rateAt). Change a step target only through SetArtefactStepTarget (Attach/SetArtefactsRecipe/MCP already do): it records the row, re-syncs the pivot and repays closed sessions from the effective day. Writing the pivot rate directly leaves history and pay out of step. A date applies from the start of that day in the organisation's timezone; no date = today, or the start of this pay week for a step that had no target; future days are refused. Days in a pay week locked for payroll (before ManufacturePayrollAdjustment::lockedBefore) are refused and recalculation never touches sessions there (Raul, 3 Nov 2026): paid weeks are settled, the accountant corrects underpayments by hand. rateAt takes the row set last (highest id) among those already in force, so a target dated back behind a later change replaces it from its day on. Removing a step records a null target (SetArtefactStepTarget::recordRemoval in DetachManufactureTaskFromArtefact), so a step added back starts without its old target.

## Payroll lock: route every pay-changing edit through WithManufacturePayrollLock
A pay week (Mon–Sun) locks at the end of the Monday after it in the organisation's time. ManufacturePayrollAdjustment::lockedBefore() returns that Monday's DATE at 00:00 stored (UTC) time, because payroll puts a session in the week of its stored ended_at date: compare moments to it, never convert it to local time. Any action that changes hours, units or pay of a past session, break, artisan day or Guru dates must wrap its write in changeInPayWeek() (trait WithManufacturePayrollLock): open weeks only for supervisors/the production manager; locked weeks only for accounting.{org}.edit, with lock_reason, recorded in manufacture_payroll_adjustments (append-only, audited) with the pay delta, settled by GetManufacturePayroll in the run whose Tuesday–Monday window holds the change (settledIn()), and the accountant notified. Roles: accountant = accounting.{org}.edit WITHOUT org-supervisor; production manager = organisation admin (org-supervisor); floor supervisors = productions_operations.{id}.orchestrate. Use startOfWeek(Carbon::MONDAY): plain startOfWeek() follows the locale (en_US starts on Sunday). Clocked hours are the artisans' paid hours, so every human action that adds, edits or deletes a clocking, time tracker, timesheet or approves a clocking adjustment goes through ChangeArtisanClocking::run() (wrapped at asController for HR screens; warehouse-team screens pass a null editor so they can never touch a locked week).

## Edited pay band tiers: write through SetManufactureStepTiers
HELP-4465 (3 Nov 2026): band 1–3 thresholds typed by hand for a recipe step live in manufacture_step_tiers (append-only, one row per change with previous_thresholds and user), per artefact or per family. Write them only through SetManufactureStepTiers (Production Manager = org-supervisor, or accounting.{org}.edit); read them with ManufactureStepTier::inForce at the session's started_at: artefact row, then family row, then target x band multiplier. A row only counts while every band is above the session's target (isAboveTarget): a target raised past edited tiers falls through to the family's, then the calculated ones, instead of paying a band for work below the target (QA, 3 Nov 2026). A row with null thresholds is a reset. CalculateManufactureTaskSessionPay snapshots what it used on the session as tier_thresholds (Performance shows it); combined batches and hourly artisans keep calculated tiers. They apply from the start of the day saved and only today's sessions are paid again, filtered by lockedBefore like SetArtefactStepTarget rather than wrapped in changeInPayWeek: earlier days stay as paid (Raul).

## Carried and split job lines share their order's work: set step quantities through CreditOrderWorkToJobOrderItem
HELP-4509 (5 Nov 2026): an order is the root job order plus every job with data->carried_from = root, splits included (JobOrderItem::orderItems). Work made on a step beyond a part's needs (mix in kg above all) belongs to the order, so a carried or split line asks only for nominal minus the order's surplus: surplus = Σ made − Σ (other lines' recipe need − what they will still make themselves). Never reset a line's steps to requiredFor(quantity) directly when its quantity changes (UpdateJobOrderItem, SplitJobOrderItem, SettleShortJobOrderItemTask all go through CreditOrderWorkToJobOrderItem), or a credited MIX step is asked again and locks Making. Overproduction a manager authorises on one line is taken off the order's other open lines (newest first, never below 1, skipping lines being worked or in a combined batch, with the To Produce lines it covers) in CloseManufactureTaskSession::takeOffRestOfOrder, so the order keeps its ordered total; the overproduction entry records taken_off_lines and CorrectManufactureTaskSessionQuantity gives them back when the extra is corrected away. A line carried on or no longer open counts all its recipe needs against the surplus (it makes nothing more).

## Moving an entry to another line grows the line like overproduction and records it to undo
HELP-4526 (5 Nov 2026): CorrectManufactureTaskSessionQuantity::moveToStep moves a finished entry to any step of a CONFIRMED, not received line of the same production, with a required reason, recorded in data.corrections on both jobs. A line without room (closed short) grows through CloseManufactureTaskSession::growLine: the moved step and later steps only, never earlier ones (UpdateJobOrderItem/CreditOrderWorkToJobOrderItem would reopen a finished mix). The growth is kept in the target job's data.line_growths {session_id, job_order_item_id, from, to}; moving that entry away again or lowering it shrinks the line back (undoLineGrowth), never below what is made. It takes nothing off the rest of the order (the goods were made on that line). Lines with data.carried_to never grow.

## A recipe step is its row, not its task: the same task can repeat with a label
HELP-4544 (5 Nov 2026): a recipe can hold the same manufacture task more than once, told apart by artefacts_manufacture_tasks.label (Mixing – Red / Mixing – Blue); identity within a recipe is (task, label), case-insensitive (ArtefactManufactureTask::key). Use Artefact::recipeSteps() for step identity; manufactureTasks() collapses repeats under find()/contains('id')/mapWithKeys(id)/sync. Job steps carry artefact_manufacture_task_id (unique per job line, null once the step leaves the recipe) and label (kept after removal; a re-added step with the same task+label adopts it). Step targets are per (artefact, task, label) in artefact_step_targets.label, so strands have their own targets and a removed-then-re-added step keeps the history past sessions were paid against; renaming a label (AttachManufactureTaskToArtefact::followLabel) relabels job steps and targets. waits_for on a step: null = earlier blocking steps (blocks_later_steps), [] = nothing, [ids] = only those earlier steps (blockingStep, refusePastPreviousStepOfCarriedJob). Show names through stepName(). Traps: Eloquent\Collection::only() filters by model key, use toBase()->only(); Model::refresh() does not reload Pivot relations, so JobOrderItemTask::refresh() unsets recipeStep.

## A mix and its strand's Making are shared by every open line that uses them: go through ShareMixPool
HELP-4562 (6 Nov 2026): a kg step's mix is keyed by ArtefactManufactureTask::mixPoolKey (task + label + mix batch + raw materials per kg); a labelled step (Mixing – Light Blue) is shared across products and job orders of a production, an unlabelled one only between lines of the same artefact (most kg steps list no raw materials, so unlabelled "Mixing" of ABP-01 and FBB-08 must never pool). The step right after a labelled mix in its strand (Making – Light Blue) is shared too (ShareMixPool::poolKey, key contains `|make|`). Closing a shared step marks the session is_combined with mix_pool_key; ShareMixPool::shareOut gives the session's line first, then open lines in plan order, each only what it still needs, never as manager-authorised overproduction. Mix is made fresh and all of it is made into pieces (Martina): mix kg not shared is never given to later lines; the extra pieces of a Making session are the strand's balance (session quantity − Σ shares, never stored), given first to the next lines needing the strand (ShareMixPool::fill), each with its mix step marked done from the mix it came from (mixFor, past the mix's leftover if need be). JobOrderItemTask::setRequiredQuantity calls ShareMixPool::settle for shared steps: a line asking less gives the excess back (CreditOrderWork floors shared steps at 0), except a line carried to another job (it keeps it for HELP-4509 credit; SettleShort lowers carried shared steps without settle). Raw materials of a shared step leave stock when it is recorded (TakeMixRawMaterialsFromStock, put back on void/correction); ReceiveJobOrderIntoStock skips a step when any line of its order (root + carried parts) has a shared share on it. A shared session that went into a received line or a job no longer open cannot be voided or corrected (ShareMixPool::settledLineOf); a correction re-shares to the lines it went to first. Cancelled lines and lines sent to the backlog give back what they have not used (CancelJobOrder::unusedMixSteps: nothing later in the strand, or in an unlabelled step like Packing, was worked). Never write shares or quantity_made by hand. Writes take a pg advisory lock per pool key (Making key, then mix key).

## Bottles filled to order: one artefact per size, matched by product code
HELP-4556 (6 Nov 2026): an artefact with settings.fills_size (e.g. "10ml") and no org_stock fills every product coded "{its single SKO code}-{size}" (FillToOrder::artefactFor), so new oils need no setup. SubmitOrder → StoreToProduceItemsFromOrder makes one To Produce line per such order line (quantity = bottles, org_stock = the bulk oil, no shortfall check); the job line carries data.fill {to_produce_id, oil, order}. SendOrderToWarehouse holds the whole order while any fill line is open (waitingForFilling); CloseManufactureTaskSession calls FillToOrder::release, which receives the line without stock, closes the To Produce line and sends the order once nothing is left to fill. The oil still leaves the bulk SKO at picking. ReceiveJobOrderIntoStock skips fill lines. Fill lines never take oil surplus (FulfilToProduceItemsFromSurplus), carry over with data.fill, follow order line edits/removal (followOrderLine) and cancellation (dismissForCancelledOrder) until on a job; a short fill adds 'only X of Y filled' to the order note; void/correct of a released fill is refused (refuseAfterRelease).

## Moving an entry with manager-authorised extra takes the extra off the source job
HELP-4589 (9 Nov 2026): CorrectManufactureTaskSessionQuantity::moveToStep no longer refuses an entry with data.overproductions on its job. After the session leaves the source step, giveBackAuthorisedExtra drops that overproduction entry, lowers the source step's required quantity and line by the extra, and gives back taken_off_lines to the rest of the order (the same path correct() uses when an entry is lowered). The target grows through growLine as usual. The move's correction records authorised_extra_removed. A move to another step of the same line keeps the extra (the line holds those goods), and a move into a line listed in the entry's taken_off_lines is refused (growLine plus give-back would over-ask it). UpdateJobOrderItem::action skips its 'already made' validator, so these guards are the only protection. Void and re-enter is not an option when the right step is already done, so don't add the refusal back.

## Seen on a job left running past the shift is display-only, never a resolve

## A job finished short is closed in whole packs; loose pieces are derived, never stored

Never store a loose count.

# Manufacture Artisan Day

## Artisan days are recalculated from sessions whenever they are viewed
manufacture_artisan_days rows are rebuilt by HydrateManufactureArtisanDays for the period (max 92 days) whenever Daily hours, Performance or Payroll is opened. Never write their figures anywhere else. A supervisor's decision (resolution) only counts while resolved_minutes == recorded_minutes, so editing a session's times reopens the day. Overtime is now answered through manufacture_overtimes (HELP-4597, see manufacture-overtime.md); the day's own approved/not_approved resolution is kept only for days answered before it. A day belongs to the shift it started in (night shifts go to the evening they began), and expected time = shift length minus the day's unpaid_break_minutes (work_schedule_days, default 30, HELP-4281). Timed WorkScheduleBreaks are not counted: they only mark when somebody is away (chat availability). The profile break is never deducted from pay (HR Paid time and production hours take off clocked breaks only); a day that would otherwise match the shift but has no ManufactureBreak between its first job and its last is flagged no_break, and a supervisor answers it with `checked` (HELP-4285).

## Days and payroll are on clocked time (HELP-4462)
The day is checked, and payroll is paid, on the time clocked: the timesheet working_duration (closed clock in/out pairs, so clocked breaks are already out), read through HydrateManufactureArtisanDays::clockings for the employees who used the tablet in the period. Over/under, the supervisor's answer (resolved_minutes) and not authorised overtime follow clocked_minutes; recorded_minutes stays the tablet time beside it. Payroll pays each day's clocked hours: the tablet hours split into bands as before, clocked time not on the tablet at band 0, and tablet time beyond the clocked time unpaid, taken off the jobs that ended last and flagged on the day (tablet_over_clocked_minutes, its own warning, cleared by correcting the job times or the clocking). Hourly artisans get their clocked hours at band 0 with no speed bands, but machine maintenance (band 2) and development (D/DG) keep their own rate for every pay type. The Guru bonus is on clocked hours. Workers come from the period's sessions plus HydrateManufactureArtisanDays::rosterEmployeeIds (a closed session in the production in the 8 weeks up to the period end, minus those hidden from the artisan roster), so hiding someone from the roster on To produce also stops their clocked-only days being paid from factory payroll. A day not clocked pays nothing.

## Days and payroll read sessions through HydrateManufactureArtisanDays::artisanSessions
HELP-4629 (10 Nov 2026): one stray tablet session put a warehouse supervisor and a marketer of another organisation into factory payroll with their whole clocked week. HydrateManufactureArtisanDays::artisanSessions($production) is the one place that says whose sessions count: employees of the production's own organisation who are not in data.hidden_artisan_ids (sessions with no employee stay). The day hydrator, rosterEmployeeIds and GetManufacturePayroll all start from it, so hiding someone (To produce > By artisan, the x on their name) removes their tablet time and their clocked hours from days and payroll, including the week of the session. Never query manufacture_task_sessions directly for hours or pay.

## A paid time rule can limit pay to the shift: read paid time through HydrateManufactureArtisanDays::paidTime
HELP-4629 (10 Nov 2026): "payroll pays the time clocked" (above) holds only while no ManufacturePaidTimeRule limits the day. A rule (manufacture_paid_time_rules, per production, dated, set on Daily hours through SetManufacturePaidTimeRule by org-supervisor or accounting edit, never dated into a locked week, never seeded: other factories keep paying clocked time) with inside_shift_only pays the clocked time between shift start and end, a late first clock in counted from the nearest rounding_minutes step after the shift start, plus time outside the shift up to the approved overtime's approved_minutes; a day off pays approved overtime only; no working hours or no closed trackers = clocked time paid. paidTime() is the only place that works this out, from time_trackers (closed, not deleted), and GetManufacturePayroll, the day row (paid_seconds, null = clocked is paid), SetManufactureOvertime (approving approves all minutes outside the shift) and ManufactureOvertime::toilBalances all use it. Flags (over/under, tablet over clocked) stay on the real clocked time. Work schedules are not dated, so for a day in a locked week the shift is always the one kept on its day row (keptShift, in paidTime and in hydrateDay; no working hours then stays no working hours): a later change of working hours must never re-price a paid week, while clocking corrections there still flow through changeInPayWeek. Tablet time worked outside the shift and not paid comes off those jobs first (capToClocked). The payroll column once called Clocked hours is Paid hours; clocked_hours and outside_shift_hours sit beside it. The overtime an approval covers under the rule is the time outside the shift it pays (paidTime 'overtime', kept on the day as paid_overtime_seconds), not paid minus expected: somebody in early and out early has no time over the shift, yet the early hour is weekend rate or TOIL once approved; GetManufacturePayroll::withOvertime and toilBalances both read that figure. Night shifts (crossing midnight) and a working day with no start or end time are not limited: their clocked time is paid. SetManufactureOvertime approves at least the planned window, so an approval saved while the day is still running is not stored as 0 minutes.

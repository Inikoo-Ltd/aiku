Hard rule: it reads only offers for every customer (no customer_id, no offer_has_customers row, no voucher code, not discretionary; customer offers never), gifts only when trigger_type is Shop (a range gift is not "a free gift with your order"), and never gives an offer name (internal codes and percentages). End days are taken PROMOTION_END_MARGIN_HOURS before end_at in the shop timezone: offers ending 23:59:59Z would otherwise read as the next day east of UTC. The sale line is the shop's sales in general, never proof that a sale the customer named is still on. The cited-source rule is lifted only when shop_promotions wrote its line AND the model declared shop_answer (then the line shows alone); any other mix with an uncited shop_information still gets the person offer. The model never says whether a promotion comes back. A message of up to three words naming a country or a nationality (countries table plus COUNTRY_WORDS, style words like Tibetan or Indian left out) skips the plain search, which has no country filter. New sentences exist in the 15 storefront languages.

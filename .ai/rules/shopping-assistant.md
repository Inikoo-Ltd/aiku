# Shopping Assistant

## Assistant texts are wiped in one place, and wiped conversations are hidden from customers
HELP-4608 (12 Nov 2026): every wipe of what customers wrote to or were told by the AI shopping assistant goes through WipeShoppingAssistantConversations (message body/tool_calls/follow_ups, event reasons, hand-over questions, challenge quotes and topics; sets wiped_at; the nightly run also re-wipes text written after a wipe). AnonymiseCustomer and the nightly shopping-assistant:wipe-old (group setting shopping_assistant.retention_months, else config retention_months 24) both call it; add any new text column there. Every customer-facing lookup of a conversation (ask, hand-over, poll, history list) must exclude wiped_at like CLEARED. Challenge tagging (Haiku, nightly) is not a shopping_assistant_model_calls row so it never counts against a website budget. Weekly summary recipients are user ids in group settings (shopping_assistant.weekly_summary_user_ids), never names in code.

## Shop memory (customers.shop_profile) is kept until the customer clears it
Raul, 12 Nov 2026 (HELP-4606): what the assistant remembers about a shop (customers.shop_profile, written by the remember_about_shop tool) is NOT wiped by the conversation retention period (WipeShoppingAssistantConversations). It stays until the customer presses "Forget this" on the Range Advisor panel or the customer is anonymised (AnonymiseCustomer). It never holds figures (the tool refuses digits, so the reply number guard holds), is audit-excluded on Customer, and the prompt presents it as facts the customer gave, not instructions. Cheeky jokes in the personal touch are for customers.is_staff only.

## MER tickets are only confirmed problems only engineers can fix
Raul, 13 Nov 2026: Merlin (report_to_engineers → StoreShoppingAssistantEngineerTicket) raises a MER ticket straight to the engineers ("the Archmages"), skipping customer services, only when the customer tried the documented fix, Merlin gives the evidence (why_only_us) and an independent AskJev check is >= MER_CONFIDENCE sure it is our defect. Anything unsure raises NO ticket (no CUS fallback): Merlin offers a person in chat and CS raise a ticket themselves if real. No noise in MER. One open MER per customer; repeats become comments. Works on B2B and dropshipping shops; dropshipping tickets carry the sales channel counters.

## Three models, and the premium one is rare by design
Raul, 13 Nov 2026: Merlin answers on the main model (Opus). AskShoppingAssistant::modelFor asks AskJev about each typed message of three words or more and moves off the main model only at routing_confidence or above: chat unrelated to the business runs on light_model (Haiku, Merlin may play along briefly), and premium_model (Fable) is only for a long enough hard question from a big spender (lifetime sales in group currency, lower bar for Gold Reward members, invoiced in the last 12 months), a customer hesitating on the point of ordering, or one retry when the main model was about to say it is not sure. Never widen the premium triggers to whole conversations or all customers: a premium turn costs about three times a main one. A turn that already acted (basket, team question, lead, engineer report, clear) is never rerun on another model, and a failed light or premium pass falls back to the main model's answer. Merlin has two goals, answering the customer and making the sale, with at most one unpushy suggestion per reply. Tests switch light_model and premium_model off in beforeEach; a test that needs routing turns them on and mocks AskJev.

## Merlin answers where an order is, and tells a customer nobody answered when the team is back
Raul, 14 Nov 2026. order_status reads only the signed-in customer's own orders (by our reference, their own or their store's, else the last one placed). The model gets the state in words and words it in the customer's language; the order number, date, carrier, tracking number and link go under the reply as one line built by code, so no translation is needed and no figure comes from the model. Payments, invoices, refunds, changes and delivery problems still go to a person (order_question); an offer after order_status keeps the order line above it and uses the sentence that does not say "I cannot see your orders". TellShoppingAssistantCustomersTeamIsAway (every 5 minutes) tells a customer once, after WAIT_MINUTES, when a live-chat hand-over or a question to our team got no reply: when the team is back outside chat hours, that nobody is free inside them. It reuses the hand-over's out-of-hours sentences, which exist in every storefront language; add a new sentence there only with its 15 translations. Raul wants honesty over a promise nobody keeps; it creates no staff task and closes nothing.

## A product code or a plain product search is answered from the search, with no model call
Raul, 14 Nov 2026 ("save money providing the best chats possible, only if quality still good"). In the first days 124 of 275 turns were one to three words with no question mark and cost over half the spend; where the model answered them with one search, every product it showed came from that search. AskShoppingAssistant::productCodeTurn answers a message that is only the code of a product in stock, and plainSearchTurn answers one to three words Jev reads as only naming what to see (at routing_confidence), by running search_products as the model would and writing one fixed sentence (model 'product-code' / 'plain-search', flags product_code / plain_search, cost 0). Everything that needs explaining stays with the model: an unknown, out of stock or discontinued code, a question ("samples"), an unsure reading, fewer than PLAIN_SEARCH_MIN_PRODUCTS results, or words that answer a question Merlin just asked. Switch: services.shopping_assistant.plain_search (tests turn it off in beforeEach). The fixed sentences exist in the 15 storefront languages; add a new one only with its translations. GetShoppingAssistantBudget::projections is for the report only (month spend plus the last 7 days' daily average for the days left), never on the path of a customer's question.

## The AI answers a customer only when it is very sure; otherwise the question goes to the guru
Raul, 14 Nov 2026 (INI-257). Applies to Merlin live and to the researcher routine that works the questions Merlin could not answer. An answer needs evidence it can name (data it queried, the code, a published guide, a cited fact); likely is not proven; a question whose meaning has to be guessed is not answered; policy, exceptions, promises and figures not in the data are never answered by the AI. Not sure = the guru, with what was looked at. A defect or missing data = a ticket to engineers. Do not loosen the cited-source rule, the number guard or the confidence bars to raise the share of questions answered: a wrong answer sent and saved as a fact is worse than a late one.

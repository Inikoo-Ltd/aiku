# Deposit Request

## Money requests: every amount on its own line, no wallet line
Vika, 9 Nov 2026: an agent's money request never asks for "the negative balance" (kind wallet is legacy, refused by StoreAgentMoneyRequest). Each amount is its own line: PO advance/balance (covered by what was asked only, closed POs ask what was paid beyond that), shipment charges, and expense (one ledger entry without shipment, or a supplier payment without PO, linked by agent_payment_id). The client's money held by the agent and never asked for (transfers outside a request and not against a shipment, supplier refunds, plus legacy wallet lines) is taken off by the server as one credit item with a NEGATIVE amount, so sum(items.amount) is always what the client sends; approving a request with nothing outstanding settles it. Agents add ad-hoc costs (bank charges) through StoreAgentMoneyRequestExpense, which writes an other_expense ledger entry with required description and optional receipt (the agent sometimes asks for the money before paying).

## The client sets approved money requests as paid in one transfer
HELP-4864 (Raul, 21 Nov 2026): staff paid the wrong amount because the money tab showed the wallet balance and a per-row Pay. The client now records ONE bank transfer for all approved open requests from the to-pay box (PayAgentMoneyRequests, route grp.models.org_agent.money_requests.pay, button "Set as paid"): it locks them, splits the amount oldest first (requested_at, id) through PayAgentMoneyRequest::recordTransfer, and splits original_amount in proportion (the last part takes the remainder, never below 0). It needs ApproveAgentMoneyRequest::canPay on every approved request. Rows have no Pay button; the single-request route stays for API use. On screen an open request is "Waiting for approval" or "Approved" (IndexAgentMoneyRequests::shownState, from approved_at; state stays requested in the database), settled shows as "Paid", rejected as "Declined" (Decline button). Unapprove was removed: Decline covers a mistaken approval. Due shows only once approved. The to-pay box (ShowOrgAgent::toPay) sums only approved requests, on every page.

## Agent money requests are paid from the client's bank feed (MatchAgentMoneyTransfers)
HELP-4864 (Raul, 21 Nov 2026). Outgoing booked bank_transactions of the client org (any bank) to a bank_payee linked to the agent (model Agent, or the agent's Organisation) settle its approved money requests through PayAgentMoneyRequests, with agent_payments.bank_transaction_id + data.bank_line_key (connection:wise transfer id, survives line replacement) standing as the bank statement. Amount = what the agent received in its currency (Wise data.transfer.targetValue, else the line amount when sent in that currency). Exact total due (one line or all lines together) records on its own after every sync and on approval; anything else waits on the Money tab for a one-click Yes (cross-currency Yes pays the whole due, amount sent kept as original) or "Not this one" (key kept in org_agents.settings.agent_money_dismissed, never in bank match_data, which matching resets). Window: from the day before the oldest approved open request (Fio/Tatra book at local midnight) and after the last transfer_received with no bank line; any such transfer inside the window or up to 7 days before it (client by hand or agent ledger; Fio/Tatra book days late) stops automatic recording, so the same money is never counted twice. Several lines each equal to the due wait for a person. Yes posts the amount shown and is refused if the due changed. Undo (UndoAgentMoneyBankTransfer, from the Money tab's "Set as paid from your bank" list, which flags lines the bank reversed or changed) soft-deletes every transfer of that bank line through DeleteAgentPayment::handle, reopening its requests, and puts the key in org_agents.settings.agent_money_undone: the line is offered again for a click but never recorded on its own again. Lines set aside stay listed and can still be paid. Every matcher write locks the org_agent row first.

# Goods In

## Service invoices own the checklist rows they fill
Local container bills (freight, duty, import VAT, other) live in stock_delivery_service_invoices, split per container in stock_delivery_service_invoice_allocations, never on the agent/supplier invoice. SyncStockDeliveryServiceInvoiceCosts writes the checklist through Store/Update/DeleteStockDeliveryCost: each bill type maps to a checklist row through StockDeliveryServiceInvoiceTypeEnum::costType(): shipping = freight + local transport bills on top of the agent invoice's freight (stock_deliveries.data.agent_invoice_freight, never replaced by a bill), duty = customs duty + anti-dumping + other import taxes (org currency), one extra row per bill of any other type (stock_delivery_service_invoice_id). A new bill type only needs a case, a label and its costType; unofficial payments stay in "other", never a type of their own, rows flagged from_service_invoices and locked against hand edits. Import VAT is recoverable and never enters costing (Raul, 9 Oct 2026). A bill touching a costed delivery can't change money until Update costing reopens it.

## Costs never hold the warehouse back; an outstanding invoice is an estimate, never a half-applied costing
Raul, 11 Oct 2026 (HELP-3844). A cost row waiting for its invoice that carries an amount is an estimate (is_estimate, set only by StoreStockDeliveryCost::withWaiting): it counts, the delivery completes and stock is valued once; the real invoice replaces it through Update costing, accounting manager only. Do not build a repeatable "apply costs so far to stock": it leaks later edits into put-aways and leaves stock history stale.
Raul, 23 Oct 2026 (HELP-4261) replaced the HELP-3844 put-away block: the warehouse books in as fast as it can and never sees, enters or is asked about costs. Receiving and put-away are never refused for missing costs, and the warehouse page of a delivery gets no costing props (costing, invoice_costing, service_invoices, agentInvoice, agreed_term_differences are null/empty on ShowStockDelivery::inWarehouse). Costing is procurement's job, enforced on procurement: StockDelivery::costsMissing() (invoice, shipping, duty need amount, estimate or N/A; not costed, not cancelled/not received, created from COSTS_REQUIRED_FROM, partner deliveries exempt) is checked when UpdateStockDeliveryStateFromGoodsIn moves a delivery from received/checked to booking in or booked in, and ReportStockDeliveryBookedInWithoutCosts (queued after commit, once per delivery via the booked_in_without_costs audit) notifies the procurement clerks and supervisors of the organisation (by role, not permission: org and group admins hold procurement edit and are not procurement staff) and the supply-chain role by app and email. GetStockDeliveriesCostingReminder shares a modal, to procurement clerks and supervisors only, on the first grp page of each day (never on the clocking_employees pages) while any booking-in/booked-in/placed delivery misses costs; it stays until acknowledged, and one waiting more than 5 working days needs a reason, audited as costing_late_reason, and may be left out of the reminder until a date at most a month ahead (data.costing_reminder_snoozed_until). Put-away is valued at org_net_amount until costed; EvaluateStockDeliveryCosting then reprices the movements and rebuilds the daily stock histories from the first repriced day (every costing, not only Update costing). Keep scopeWithCostsMissing in step with costsMissing().
stock_delivery_costs rows are soft deleted and audited, one live row per non-extra type (partial unique index); an extra needs a category from StockDeliveryServiceInvoiceTypeEnum::extraOptions(). Typing an amount means received: there is no Received tick.

## A model reading a costing document proposes, code checks, a person applies; the amounts are never edited on the way
Raul, 13 Oct 2026 (HELP-3911): costing from documents has no room for errors. ReadStockDeliveryServiceInvoice has two models of different makers (services.stock_delivery_documents.readers) read the document apart; the reading is stored on the attachment (model_has_attachments.data.invoice_reading, kind service_invoice) and never writes a cost. StockDeliveryDocumentChecks and GetStockDeliveryServiceInvoiceReadings::review refuse it when the readers disagree, an amount is not printed in the document text, the document names another delivery, lines do not add up to the total, reference/date/currency is missing, the reference is already a service invoice, or it exceeds the goods value. ApplyStockDeliveryServiceInvoiceReading re-runs the checks on the stored reading and takes only the type of each line from the reviewer: do not add amount fields to the apply request, a refused reading is entered by hand with StoreStockDeliveryServiceInvoice. A document with no text (scan, photo) needs the reviewer's tick. A document that does not print the delivery's reference is not refused (HELP-3923, forwarders write our references their own way): the reviewer is shown what it prints and whether its date fits the delivery (from the day it left to 90 days after it arrived), and must type the confirmation phrase, checked again in ApplyStockDeliveryServiceInvoiceReading and kept on the reading as delivery_confirmed. Apply checks and writes inside one transaction holding a lock on the stock delivery row, and the read job never overwrites a reading that was applied: keep both, they are what stops a double click or a late re-read from booking the invoice twice. Any new reader (customs declaration, agent invoice, the MCP door) goes through the same checks, not around them.
The supplier/agent goods invoice follows it since 20 Oct 2026 (INI-018): ReadStockDeliveryInvoice (kind supplier_invoice) has the same two readers and checks (lines compared as code+quantity+amount, charges and total too); GetStockDeliveryInvoiceCosting::review lists every problem (lines+charges must equal the printed total, a delivery product missing from the invoice, an invoice unit price more than 20% from the order unit price (packs/cartons; a received quantity that differs is NOT a problem: cost is invoice price x received, and the money for goods not received stays out of stock value for a supplier claim, shown as not_received/not_invoiced; tested on the real Sheikh-046 invoice 20 Oct, where hand costing had loaded order amounts onto swapped and short counts), a discount or credit, an invoice already on another delivery of the same supplier or already recorded on this one, state before checked) and ApplyStockDeliveryInvoiceCosting re-runs the review under the row lock and takes only where unmatched lines go (an item or not_in_delivery, never "leave out") and each charge's extra category, never an amount. It applies from checked (UpdateStockDeliveryItemCost allows action() calls before placed; put-away is valued at org_net_amount until costed, so early cost_items are safe). Readings made before 20 Oct (no kind) must be read again. StockDeliveryItem.unit_quantity_checked defaults to 0, not null: decide "received" by the delivery state.
INI-120 (Raul, 20 Oct 2026): invoice lines match by supplier product code, then our SKO code, then barcode (org_stocks.unit_barcode/barcode, stocks.carton_barcode), each only when it names exactly one delivery line; a line's barcode is kept only when both readers read the same one (ReadStockDeliveryInvoice::barcode drops spreadsheet 5.05637E+12). A description only fills suggested_item_id: the line stays unmatched until the reviewer places it. RMB is read as CNY (StockDeliveryDocumentChecks::currencyCode), a dash amount is 0. A charge typed freight becomes the shipping like the agent invoice freight (data.agent_invoice_freight, forwarder bills add on top via SyncStockDeliveryServiceInvoiceCosts) and is refused when shipping was typed by hand. Decided to stay refused: agent containers with an agent invoice record, and invoices that print no total (AW China).

## A supplier invoice in another currency asks whether the delivery follows it
Raul, 13 Oct 2026 (HELP-3923). ApplyStockDeliveryInvoiceCosting needs follow_invoice when the invoice and the delivery differ in currency. Yes runs ChangeStockDeliveryCurrency: the delivery moves to the invoice's currency, item amounts are converted and the exchange rates follow so org and group amounts do not change, costs typed before keep their amount and old currency with the rate, purchase orders stay as placed (UpdatePurchaseOrdersCostFromStockDelivery converts through the org exchange). It is refused while payments, an applied deposit, an agent invoice, a supplier invoice record or claims exist in the old currency: add a conversion for them before lifting a blocker. No keeps the delivery's currency, records the invoice in its own with the reviewer's rate, and puts the rounding cents on the largest line. The invoice never adds or removes items: a line that matches none is an extra expense of category not_in_delivery, which is never a service invoice type (StockDeliveryServiceInvoiceTypeEnum::billTypes()).

## Update costing: the accounting manager may name who makes the update
Raul, 22 Oct 2026 (HELP-4256). Only an accounting manager can reopen a costed delivery (ReopenStockDeliveryCosting), but they may pass assignee_id: someone with procurement or accounting edit in the org (ReopenStockDeliveryCosting::assignableUsers, wildcard parents included). It is stored in data.costing_reopened.assignee_id and dies when the costing is finished. Every check on a reopened costing goes through StockDelivery::canUpdateCosting($user) (the edit trait, Finish, UpdateStockDelivery org_exchange, ShowStockDelivery, the MCP costs tool and McpChange::canBeRevertedBy): never check org-supervisor accounting by hand there, or the assignee is locked out.

## Agents with their own warehouse ship only what goods receipts accepted
HELP-4292 (26 Oct 2026). Agent setting settings.receives_goods_in_own_warehouse (Agent::receivesGoodsInOwnWarehouse, StoreStockDeliveryFromPurchaseOrder::shipsFromAgentWarehouse). When on: every stock delivery line is capped in StoreStockDeliveryFromPurchaseOrder at units accepted on RECEIVED agent goods receipts minus units on live deliveries (whereReadyToShip / withQuantityReadyToShip); all container-filling paths must keep going through that action. Goods receipts then accept any open line whatever stage the PO is at (IndexAgentProductsInProduction::inProductionStageSql), or goods would be stuck after a first lot ships or when production was never marked. Handed over is hidden and refused (MarkPurchaseOrderStage); the Clean Handover Score uses the goods receipt that completed the order instead. Only set it on agents that record receipts (Nepal), not Indo/China without a plan: their open POs would stop shipping.

## Costing is mistake-proof: every Upload is read, amounts take cents, the closer can undo for 2 working days
Raul, 26 Oct 2026 (HELP-4329: a container's shipping was typed 6.938 EUR for 6,938 and closed at once). The Upload button of the agent invoice, shipping and duty checklist rows (AttachStockDeliveryCostInvoice) files the document on the delivery and queues the AI reader (ReadStockDeliveryInvoice / ReadStockDeliveryServiceInvoice::queue) unless it was read already; only an extra keeps its file on the row. A person types at most 2 decimals (StoreStockDeliveryCost::amountRules, request only; actions keep full precision). EvaluateStockDeliveryCosting stores data.costed {at, user_id} when a costing closes; for 2 working days (addWeekdays(2): costed Friday afternoon, fixed Monday morning) that user can reopen it without an accounting manager (StockDelivery::canUndoCosting) and becomes its assignee. Reopen/Finish work from any state but cancelled/not received (isCostingReopenable), not only placed.

## Every invoice path feeds its charges into the costing
HELP-4444 (30 Oct 2026). Agent invoices (ApplyAgentInvoiceCosting), read supplier invoices (ApplyStockDeliveryInvoiceCosting) and hand-entered supplier invoices (StoreActualStockDeliveryInvoice::chargesToCosting, on first entry only) all turn invoice charges into checklist rows: freight -> shipping + data.agent_invoice_freight, other charges -> extras. The hand path skips the freight when shipping was typed by hand instead of refusing it. StockDelivery.vue subtracts min(invoice charges, shipping + extra) from the landed costs on the assumption that the charges are already in those rows; a path that skips the feed leaves the freight out of the stock value and hides real extras (a customs clearance vanished from the landed total on SDes-0035).

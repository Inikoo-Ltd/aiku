# Goods In

## Service invoices own the checklist rows they fill
Local container bills (freight, duty, import VAT, other) live in stock_delivery_service_invoices, split per container in stock_delivery_service_invoice_allocations, never on the agent/supplier invoice. SyncStockDeliveryServiceInvoiceCosts writes the checklist through Store/Update/DeleteStockDeliveryCost: each bill type maps to a checklist row through StockDeliveryServiceInvoiceTypeEnum::costType(): shipping = freight + local transport bills on top of the agent invoice's freight (stock_deliveries.data.agent_invoice_freight, never replaced by a bill), duty = customs duty + other import taxes (org currency), one extra row per bill of any other type (stock_delivery_service_invoice_id), anti-dumping included (see below). A new bill type only needs a case, a label and its costType; unofficial payments stay in "other", never a type of their own, rows flagged from_service_invoices and locked against hand edits. Import VAT is recoverable and never enters costing (Raul, 9 Oct 2026). A bill touching a costed delivery can't change money until Update costing reopens it.

## Anti-dumping duty goes only to the lines of the tariff codes picked for it
HELP-4796 (18 Nov 2026). Anti-dumping is charged on some tariff codes of a container, never all of it, so it is not part of the Duty row: it is an extra row with category anti_dumping (by hand, or one per anti-dumping service invoice) that lands in the lines' cost_duties (StockDeliveryCost::lineCostField). The user picks its tariff codes in the landed costs (stock_delivery_costs.applies_to_tariff_codes); StoreStockDeliveryCost::withTariffCodes stores the delivery lines carrying them at that moment in applies_to_item_ids, and EvaluateStockDeliveryCosting::lineAmounts splits the row by value among those lines only, to the cent. Resolved lines are kept, not re-read from trade units, so a later tariff code change never moves money in a costing. A row with no codes, or whose lines were all cancelled or not received, keeps the costing open (EvaluateStockDeliveryCosting::costsWithoutLines): never fall back to spreading it over the whole delivery. A service invoice row stays locked except for its tariff codes, and a re-sync keeps them.

## Costs never hold the warehouse back; an outstanding invoice is an estimate, never a half-applied costing
Raul, 11 Oct 2026 (HELP-3844). A cost row waiting for its invoice that carries an amount is an estimate (is_estimate, set only by StoreStockDeliveryCost::withWaiting): it counts, the delivery completes and stock is valued once; the real invoice replaces it through Update costing, accounting manager only. Do not build a repeatable "apply costs so far to stock": it leaks later edits into put-aways and leaves stock history stale.
Raul, 23 Oct 2026 (HELP-4261) replaced the HELP-3844 put-away block: the warehouse books in as fast as it can and never sees, enters or is asked about costs. Receiving and put-away are never refused for missing costs, and the warehouse page of a delivery gets no costing props (costing, invoice_costing, service_invoices, agentInvoice, agreed_term_differences are null/empty on ShowStockDelivery::inWarehouse). Costing is procurement's job, enforced on procurement: StockDelivery::costsMissing() (invoice, shipping, duty need amount, estimate or N/A; not costed, not cancelled/not received, created from COSTS_REQUIRED_FROM, partner deliveries exempt) is checked when UpdateStockDeliveryStateFromGoodsIn moves a delivery from received/checked to booking in or booked in, and ReportStockDeliveryBookedInWithoutCosts (queued after commit, once per delivery via the booked_in_without_costs audit) notifies the procurement clerks and supervisors of the organisation (by role, not permission: org and group admins hold procurement edit and are not procurement staff) and the supply-chain role by app and email. GetStockDeliveriesCostingReminder shares a modal, to procurement clerks and supervisors only, on the first grp page of each day (never on the clocking_employees pages) while any booking-in/booked-in/placed delivery misses costs; it stays until acknowledged, and one waiting more than 5 working days needs a reason, audited as costing_late_reason, and may be left out of the reminder until a date at most a month ahead (data.costing_reminder_snoozed_until). Put-away is valued at org_net_amount until costed; EvaluateStockDeliveryCosting then reprices the movements and rebuilds the daily stock histories from the first repriced day (every costing, not only Update costing). Keep scopeWithCostsMissing in step with costsMissing().
stock_delivery_costs rows are soft deleted and audited, one live row per non-extra type (partial unique index); an extra needs a category from StockDeliveryServiceInvoiceTypeEnum::extraOptions(). Typing an amount means received: there is no Received tick.

## A model reading a costing document proposes, code checks, a person applies; the amounts are never edited on the way
Raul, 13 Oct 2026 (HELP-3911): costing from documents has no room for errors. ReadStockDeliveryServiceInvoice has two models of different makers (services.stock_delivery_documents.readers) read the document apart; the reading is stored on the attachment (model_has_attachments.data.invoice_reading, kind service_invoice) and never writes a cost. StockDeliveryDocumentChecks and GetStockDeliveryServiceInvoiceReadings::review refuse it when the readers disagree, an amount is not printed in the document text, the document names another delivery, lines do not add up to the total, reference/date/currency is missing, the reference is already a service invoice, or it exceeds the goods value. ApplyStockDeliveryServiceInvoiceReading re-runs the checks on the stored reading and takes only the type of each line from the reviewer: do not add amount fields to the apply request, a refused reading is entered by hand with StoreStockDeliveryServiceInvoice. A document with no text (scan, photo) needs the reviewer's tick. A document that does not print the delivery's reference is not refused (HELP-3923, forwarders write our references their own way): the reviewer is shown what it prints and whether its date fits the delivery (from the day it left to 90 days after it arrived), and must type the confirmation phrase, checked again in ApplyStockDeliveryServiceInvoiceReading and kept on the reading as delivery_confirmed. Apply checks and writes inside one transaction holding a lock on the stock delivery row, and the read job never overwrites a reading that was applied: keep both, they are what stops a double click or a late re-read from booking the invoice twice. Any new reader (customs declaration, agent invoice, the MCP door) goes through the same checks, not around them.
The supplier/agent goods invoice follows it since 20 Oct 2026 (INI-018): ReadStockDeliveryInvoice (kind supplier_invoice) has the same two readers and checks (lines compared as code+quantity+amount, charges and total too); GetStockDeliveryInvoiceCosting::review lists every problem (lines+charges must equal the printed total, a delivery product missing from the invoice, an invoice unit price more than 20% from the order unit price (packs/cartons; a received quantity that differs is NOT a problem: cost is invoice price x received, and the money for goods not received stays out of stock value for a supplier claim, shown as not_received/not_invoiced; tested on the real Sheikh-046 invoice 20 Oct, where hand costing had loaded order amounts onto swapped and short counts), a discount or credit, an invoice already on another delivery of the same supplier or already recorded on this one, state before checked) and ApplyStockDeliveryInvoiceCosting re-runs the review under the row lock and takes only where unmatched lines go (an item or not_in_delivery, never "leave out") and each charge's extra category, never an amount. It applies from checked (UpdateStockDeliveryItemCost allows action() calls before placed; put-away is valued at org_net_amount until costed, so early cost_items are safe). Readings made before 20 Oct (no kind) must be read again. StockDeliveryItem.unit_quantity_checked defaults to 0, not null: decide "received" by the delivery state.
INI-120 (Raul, 20 Oct 2026): invoice lines match by supplier product code, then our SKO code, then barcode (org_stocks.unit_barcode/barcode, stocks.carton_barcode), each only when it names exactly one delivery line; a line's barcode is kept only when both readers read the same one (ReadStockDeliveryInvoice::barcode drops spreadsheet 5.05637E+12). A description only fills suggested_item_id: the line stays unmatched until the reviewer places it. RMB is read as CNY (StockDeliveryDocumentChecks::currencyCode), a dash amount is 0. A charge typed freight becomes the shipping like the agent invoice freight (data.agent_invoice_freight, forwarder bills add on top via SyncStockDeliveryServiceInvoiceCosts) and is refused when shipping was typed by hand. Decided to stay refused: agent containers with an agent invoice record, and invoices that print no total (AW China).

## A supplier invoice in another currency asks whether the delivery follows it
Raul, 13 Oct 2026 (HELP-3923). ApplyStockDeliveryInvoiceCosting needs follow_invoice when the invoice and the delivery differ in currency. Yes runs ChangeStockDeliveryCurrency: the delivery moves to the invoice's currency, item amounts are converted and the exchange rates follow so org and group amounts do not change, costs typed before keep their amount and old currency with the rate, purchase orders stay as placed (costing a delivery never writes their amounts, see the section below). It is refused while payments, an applied deposit, an agent invoice, a supplier invoice record or claims exist in the old currency: add a conversion for them before lifting a blocker. No keeps the delivery's currency, records the invoice in its own with the reviewer's rate, and puts the rounding cents on the largest line. The invoice never adds or removes items: a line that matches none is an extra expense of category not_in_delivery, which is never a service invoice type (StockDeliveryServiceInvoiceTypeEnum::billTypes()).

## Update costing: the accounting manager may name who makes the update
Raul, 22 Oct 2026 (HELP-4256). Only an accounting manager can reopen a costed delivery (ReopenStockDeliveryCosting), but they may pass assignee_id: someone with procurement or accounting edit in the org (ReopenStockDeliveryCosting::assignableUsers, wildcard parents included). It is stored in data.costing_reopened.assignee_id and dies when the costing is finished. Every check on a reopened costing goes through StockDelivery::canUpdateCosting($user) (the edit trait, Finish, UpdateStockDelivery org_exchange, ShowStockDelivery, the MCP costs tool and McpChange::canBeRevertedBy): never check org-supervisor accounting by hand there, or the assignee is locked out.

## Agents ship only what goods receipts accepted
HELP-4292 (26 Oct 2026); every agent since HELP-4890 (23 Nov 2026), when Raul removed the receives_goods_in_own_warehouse switch: a shipment is never built PO-first, an agent without a warehouse records receipts at its (possibly virtual) staging area. StoreStockDeliveryFromPurchaseOrder::shipsFromAgentWarehouse is true for every agent order: every stock delivery line is capped in StoreStockDeliveryFromPurchaseOrder at units accepted on RECEIVED agent goods receipts minus units on live deliveries (whereReadyToShip / withQuantityReadyToShip); all container-filling paths must keep going through that action. Goods receipts then accept any open line whatever stage the PO is at (IndexAgentProductsInProduction::inProductionStageSql), or goods would be stuck after a first lot ships or when production was never marked. With po_journey.cho_mode next_container, Handed over is hidden and refused (MarkPurchaseOrderStage); the Clean Handover Score uses the goods receipt that completed the order instead. INI-174 (2 Nov 2026): settings.goods_receipts_required_from (Y-m-d, UpdateAgent) lets an agent switch over without stopping shipments: every line of a PO created before that date counts as received in full, receipts or not (quantityReceivedAtAgentWarehouse, the one subquery every path reads): a receipt for the rest of a line that partly shipped under the fallback must not cap it below what already shipped. The HELP-4890 migration gave every agent that had the switch off a date (the day after it ran), so their open orders kept shipping; clean hand over mode (po_journey.cho_mode) was written down per agent as it was, so no fee moved. Agent Indo uses it for its sheet-import stock. Never give Agent China a date: it switched on without one and 779 of its open lines have no receipt, so a date would let them ship unchecked.

## Costing is mistake-proof: every Upload is read, amounts take cents, the closer can undo for 2 working days
Raul, 26 Oct 2026 (HELP-4329: a container's shipping was typed 6.938 EUR for 6,938 and closed at once). The Upload button of the agent invoice, shipping and duty checklist rows (AttachStockDeliveryCostInvoice) files the document on the delivery and queues the AI reader (ReadStockDeliveryInvoice / ReadStockDeliveryServiceInvoice::queue) unless it was read already; only an extra keeps its file on the row. A person types at most 2 decimals (StoreStockDeliveryCost::amountRules, request only; actions keep full precision). EvaluateStockDeliveryCosting stores data.costed {at, user_id} when a costing closes; for 2 working days (addWeekdays(2): costed Friday afternoon, fixed Monday morning) that user can reopen it without an accounting manager (StockDelivery::canUndoCosting) and becomes its assignee. Reopen/Finish work from any state but cancelled/not received (isCostingReopenable), not only placed.

## Every invoice path feeds its charges into the costing
HELP-4444 (30 Oct 2026). Agent invoices (ApplyAgentInvoiceCosting), read supplier invoices (ApplyStockDeliveryInvoiceCosting) and hand-entered supplier invoices (StoreActualStockDeliveryInvoice::chargesToCosting, on first entry only) all turn invoice charges into checklist rows: freight -> shipping + data.agent_invoice_freight, other charges -> extras. The hand path skips the freight when shipping was typed by hand instead of refusing it. StockDelivery.vue subtracts min(invoice charges, shipping + extra) from the landed costs on the assumption that the charges are already in those rows; a path that skips the feed leaves the freight out of the stock value and hides real extras (a customs clearance vanished from the landed total on SDes-0035).

## Costing a stock delivery never writes its purchase orders' amounts
INI-236 (12 Nov 2026), from Raul's 8 Oct 2026 ruling (INI-069): costing lives on the stock delivery and is spread over its stock_delivery_items; a purchase order is never a costing unit. UpdatePurchaseOrdersCostedFromStockDelivery only sets purchase_orders.is_costed (a delivery placed and every live delivery costed). Do not copy or split stock delivery cost_* onto purchase orders: the old copy put the whole delivery on each of its orders (AWIndia-52: 7 orders each showing the container's 4,743,776) and purchase_orders.cost_items feeds deposits and agent money (SyncPurchaseOrderDepositPaid, GetAgentMoneyRequestLines). Read landed costs from the stock delivery.

## Accepting a surplus or adding an item not ordered rewrites what the line expects
HELP-4705 (14 Nov 2026). StockDeliveryItem::orgStockMovementCost divides the line's cost by unit_quantity (expected), while invoice costing sets cost_items to the invoice price x units received. A line's expected quantity must therefore equal what we keep, or the stock is valued wrong per SKO. ResolveStockDeliveryItemDiscrepancy surplus_accepted sets unit_quantity to the checked count and net_amount to the same unit price x checked, keeping the before values in data.surplus_accepted, as unit_error_corrected does in data.unit_corrections. Both are refused on a costed delivery. AddItemNotOnPurchaseOrderToStockDelivery (goods in, received to booked_in, supplier or agent deliveries only, not costed) adds a product the supplier sent on no purchase order: expected = checked = the SKOs counted, priced at supplier_products.cost converted to the delivery's currency, with data.not_on_purchase_order. It never touches purchase orders.

## Booked in can step back; finishing with discrepancies is confirmed
HELP-4722 (16 Nov 2026). Booked in is still the warehouse's: StockDelivery::acceptsPutAwayUndo() (goods-in states + booked_in, never placed) gates DeleteSowing and the undo button (StockDeliveryItemResource can_undo_sowings), and undoing steps the delivery back via UpdateStockDeliveryStateFromGoodsIn. Booked in stays automatic on the last put-away; the web table only asks for confirmation when that put-away leaves over/under lines (finishes_booking_in_with_discrepancies from StockDelivery::bookingInLinesLeft()). The mobile API is not gated by it.

## Partner deliveries take the receiving organisation's local landed costs
Raul, 19 Nov 2026 (INI-399, GB592627 AW Ltd → SK, import duty). A partner delivery costs its goods from the partner order (no invoice row, StoreActualStockDeliveryInvoice refuses it), but shipping, duty and extras paid by the receiving organisation go on top: landed costs dialog, service invoices, readings and the MCP costs tool all accept partners. They stay optional (costsMissing and scopeToCost still exempt partners). EvaluateStockDeliveryCosting::isPartnerCosted closes a partner costing only when placed (or on Finish), with every local row received or N/A; before placed it stays open so the customs duty can be added. Costed partner deliveries are reopened with Update costing like any other (isCostingReopenable). Local costs never touch the partner invoice/refund settlement, which reads item net_amount.

## Delivery feedback is one round per delivery; item notes go to each line's supplier
HELP-4825 (Raul, 19 Nov 2026): per-supplier feedback made no sense on agent containers (SDes-0029: 36 suppliers, 220 lines). stock_delivery_feedbacks is one row per delivery (unique stock_delivery_id): the general part (stars/topics/comment/photos) is for whoever sent it, supplier_id is the delivery's own supplier on a direct delivery (even when a line comes from another supplier: QA, SDaroma-0025), else the only supplier of its lines, else null for the agent or partner (StoreStockDeliveryFeedback::addressedSupplierId). Problems with single lines are stock_delivery_item_feedbacks (one per line, from the row icon in the items table), whose supplier comes from the line's supplier product. Notes can come first: the round is a draft (rating null, sent_at null) that nobody outside sees; StoreStockDeliveryFeedback sends it and NotifyStockDeliveryFeedback tells each buyer once; a note added after sending notifies its buyer at once. A supplier portal shows the general part only when addressed to that supplier and only notes on its own lines (StockDeliveryFeedbackResource::forSupplier, photo sub_scope feedback_item:{id} checked by DownloadSupplierPortalFeedbackPhoto). Never bring back a supplier picker on the general feedback.

# Actions Procurement

## Agent warehouse: read what is held through AgentWarehouseStock::heldQuantitySql, never quantity
INI-206 (5 Nov 2026). Nothing writes agent_warehouse_stocks when goods go in a container or the container is dispatched, so a row's quantity is what was received, not what the agent still holds. Held = quantity minus the units of the same purchase_order_transaction_id on live stock delivery items (any state but cancelled/not_received, staged or dispatched), taken from the line's packed rows first, then its oldest, so a later goods receipt for the rest of a line stays held and an unpacked lot is not hidden by a packed one that shipped. PO stages keep reading the raw quantity for the packed date. Every reader of "what the agent holds" (Warehouse screens, backlog, ready checks, stages, packable quantities) uses AgentWarehouseStock::heldQuantitySql() / whereHeld() / heldQuantityColumn(). Do not add a write path that decrements rows on dispatch: undispatch, cancel and item edits would all need to restore it.

## Ordering suggestions skip seasonal SKOs that would arrive out of season
HELP-4598 (10 Nov 2026). A product-property tag can carry a season (tags.arrive_from_month / arrive_to_month, 1-12, may wrap the year end; set on the tag edit page): the months its goods should arrive in. Tag::outOfOrderingSeasonSql($stockIdColumn, $leadDays) is true for a stock whose trade units or their families carry seasonal tags, when today plus the lead time lands outside all of them, so a slow agent opens months before a fast partner on the same tag. Every query that proposes what to order applies it next to is_excluded_from_auto_ordering with that path's own lead time: SuggestPartnerShoppingList::candidates and GetPartnerStockCoverBuckets::rescuableQuery (GetPartnerLeadTime; reason out_of_season in leftOut), SuggestAgentShoppingList (GetAgentStockCoverBuckets::leadTimeExpression), SuggestSupplierShoppingList (the SKO's, the supplier product's, then the supplier median); HubOrderPlanningTool reports out_of_ordering_season. A new suggestion path must apply it too. Adding by hand, bucket counts and the recommended-quantity chips are deliberately not gated.

## A purchase order's progress is purchase_orders.state only: never compare with CONFIRMED
INI-244 (12 Nov 2026). purchase_orders.state holds the whole path: in_process, submitted, confirmed (Agreed), in_production, in_warehouse, ready, handed_over, arrived, settled, plus cancelled and not_received. There is no stage column and no bucket SQL. People draft, submit, agree, cancel; goods-in settles (syncSettlement); between confirmed and arrived PurchaseOrderHydrateState moves the state to the furthest stage GetPurchaseOrderStages reports, forward and back, and refreshes the per-state counters. A submitted order stays submitted until agreed. Never write `state === CONFIRMED` or `in ('submitted','confirmed')`: use PurchaseOrderStateEnum::open() / agreed() / live() (…Values(), …Sql(), isOpen(), isAgreed(), isLive()). Lines stop at confirmed: copy an order state onto a line with ->transactionState(). A fetch or repair that sets state confirmed on an order already past it is ignored (UpdatePurchaseOrder::keepsStateReached). A select alias named `state` or `stage` next to GROUP BY groups by the table column in Postgres, not the alias. purchase_orders.delivery_state stays: it is the containers' state, a resolver input with its own counters.

## A purchase order's total is its items; extras live on the stock delivery
INI-237 (Raul, 12 Nov 2026). CalculatePurchaseOrderTotalAmounts sets purchase_orders.cost_total to the order's items and nothing else. An order with lines in another currency than its own is the one case where the two columns differ: PurchaseOrderHydrateTransactions still overwrites cost_items with the unconverted sum of the lines while cost_total holds the converted one (found 12 Nov 2026, 111 orders, not fixed here). cost_extra, cost_shipping, cost_duties and cost_tax on purchase_orders are dead columns: nothing reads, shows, accepts or restores them (PO page, export, journey, Aurora fetch, UpdatePurchaseOrder, cancel and undo cancel). Freight, duty, agent and other landed costs are entered and seen on the stock delivery only, and agents never see them. Do not add a reader or writer of these columns. repair:purchase_order_costs (dry run unless --apply) cleared the old values and kept them in purchase_orders.data.costs_before_extras_cleared. Unit expenses (agent_unit_expense on lines, unit_expense on supplier products) are a separate estimate and never part of cost_items or cost_total.

# Ordering

## Order payment box always shows the amount left to pay, also while in the warehouse
Settled after three reversals (24 Aug hid amount and Pay button, 30 Sep HELP-3596 restored Pay, 12 Oct HELP-3896 restored the amount). While an order is in_warehouse / handling / handling_blocked (`isOrderAmountsProvisional`), `NeedToPayV2` keeps BOTH the "Need to pay X of Y" line and the Pay button. What marks the figure as provisional is the grey box, the "In warehouse" heading, the "Amounts will be recalculated when picking is finished" note and the warning above Pay, never hiding the number or the button: staff add products to orders being picked and must know what to charge. The box and the Pay window read the same `box_stats.products.payment.pay_amount` (`IsOrder`), keep it that way.

## Shop charges: read the trigger in the action, not the shop settings
What a customer pays in charges is decided by the `Calculate*Charges` actions; derive it from them, never from shop settings or a charge's description (some descriptions mention exemptions the code does not apply). `shops.settings.can_collect` is written only by `FetchAuroraShop` and read by nothing. `CalculateCollectionCharges` adds the shop's active COLLECTION charge whenever the order has a `collection_address_id`, and removes it when it has none; it has no MANUAL charges-engine check. `CalculateOrderHangingCharges` (the small-order charge) returns early when `charges_engine` is MANUAL, then matches `goods_amount` against the charge's `settings.rules` (`'<;limit'` or `'>;limit'`): `<` is strict and never applies to a goods amount of 0, so an order exactly at the limit pays nothing.

## Shipping step bands are inclusive at both ends, first match wins
`CalculateOrderShipping::getPriceBlanketFromAmount` takes the first step where `from <= amount <= to` (or `to == 'INF'` and `amount >= from`), in the order the steps are stored. Weight pricing passes `estimated_weight / 1000` (kg), so with steps 0-2 and 2-5 an order of exactly 2 kg pays the 0-2 band. The same function prices "Step Order Items Net Amount" on `goods_amount`, so net-amount bands behave the same way. When no step matches (above the last bound of a zone without an INF step, or in a gap between steps) it returns `'TBC'`, never free delivery (PRE-22: dssk zones end at 30 kg and heavier orders were invoiced €0 shipping).

## Order recalculation is re-entrant: count passes, not lines
INI-197 (4 Nov 2026). CalculateOrderTotalAmounts runs CalculateOrderDiscounts, which runs CalculateOrderTotalAmounts again with shipping on, which runs CalculateOrderShipping and CalculateOrderHangingCharges; their line writes go through StoreTransaction/UpdateTransaction, which rehydrate categories and run the totals a third time. Transaction amounts have no decimal cast, so rewriting "4.00" with 4.0 always counts as a change and cascades. The outer pass skips shipping and the hanging charge when the discount pass reports `recalculatedShippingAndCharges` (never reported for shops with `offers_data.discounted_shipping_scoped`, whose family/department scope reads categories_data that changes between the two runs); keep that flag when touching the chain. A slow basket add is extra passes, never a per-line N+1: profile by file:line before guessing. Do not drop the categories rehydrate on shipping/charge line updates: it is what stores the post-discount categories_data after an add.

## Invoicing an order reads its lines once; keep per-line work out of the loop
INI-203 (4 Nov 2026). GenerateInvoiceFromOrder::loadTransactions reads the order's transactions, their historic assets (with product brands and master product), delivery note parts, submitted sale figures and is_indivisible once; recalculateTotals (header estimate) and the line loop both price from that, and recalculateTransactionTotals falls back to its own per-line queries only for a transaction not loaded there (tests price single lines). Tax categories are checked once (checkTaxCategories) and StoreInvoiceTransaction is called with taxCategoryChecked and dispatchJobs false; one ProcessStoredInvoiceTransactions job (after commit) runs bridges then time series for all lines. A new per-line read in the loop is an N+1 again: add it to loadTransactions. Never pass the order's org_exchange/grp_exchange to StoreInvoiceTransaction: processExchanges would store order-time org/grp amounts instead of the invoice-time rate. PerLineVatTest "exactly as before the INI-203 batching" pins every line and the query budget.

## Order payment amounts are a text field read by parseAmount
HELP-4522 (5 Nov 2026): the amount in the order Payment and Return Payment dialogs is an InputText with a currency addon, read by `parseAmount` (`resources/js/Composables/parseAmount.ts`), not PrimeVue InputNumber. Staff copy amounts from bank statements as "146,17" or "1.234,56"; InputNumber under the EUR display locale took "146,17" typed or pasted as 14,617 and €14,470.83 went to a customer's balance. Keep it a text field (exception to the currency-InputNumber rule) and keep the amber line that says how much over the amount due goes to the customer's balance.

## Customer service lines parked for the warehouse stamp waiting_warehouse_released_at
HELP-4683 (12 Nov 2026). "Ready to contact customer" (IndexWaitingCrmItemsGrouped::whereReadyToContactCustomer, also used by WithTabsBox) holds a blocked note back only for waiting-warehouse lines with waiting_warehouse_released_at NULL, i.e. lines the picker parked. Any customer-service path that sends a line to the warehouse (SendBackWaitingWarehouse, ReplaceWaitingCrmItemProduct on a blocked note) must set waiting_warehouse_released_at = now(), or the note and every other missing item on it drop out of Ready until the line is picked. SetAsWaitingWarehouse (picker) clears it; SetAsWaitingCrm clears it only when nothing is left waiting in the warehouse.

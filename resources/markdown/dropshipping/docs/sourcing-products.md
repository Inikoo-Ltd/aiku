<aside class="tldr">
Every channel you open (Shopify, eBay, WooCommerce, TikTok Shop, Wix, Allegro or a manual channel) has its own product list called <b>My Products</b>. You fill it from our website or from the <b>Catalogue</b> with <b>Add to all channels</b>, or from inside the channel with <b>Add products</b>. Adding a product to <b>My Products</b> does not put it in your store yet: you still create or match it there.
</aside>

## Before you start

You need an account and at least one channel. A channel is one store or marketplace account, for example your Shopify store. Products are always added to a channel, so open a channel first: <b>Channels</b> in the menu, then the button to add a channel.

Only products that are for sale can be added. Discontinued products show <b>Product Discontinued</b> and cannot be added.

## Way 1: from our website or the Catalogue

Use this when you want to look around, see photos and read descriptions.

1. Log in. Browse our website, or open <b>Catalogue</b> in the menu. The catalogue has <b>Departments</b>, <b>Sub-Departments</b>, <b>Families</b>, <b>Products</b>, <b>Collections</b> and <b>Bundles</b>.
2. Open a product to see its price, RRP, stock and description.
3. Press <b>Add to all channels</b>. The product goes into <b>My Products</b> of every open channel you have. A channel that is not connected to its platform is skipped.
4. To add it to one channel only, press the <b>⋮</b> button next to it. Under <b>Add product to a specific channel</b>, click the channel. A tick shows the channels that already have it.

Once the product is in at least one of your channels, <b>Add to all channels</b> is replaced by a grey <b>Exist on some channels</b>: use the <b>⋮</b> button to add it to the other channels one by one. When it is in all of them the button says <b>Exist on all channels</b> and the <b>⋮</b> button goes away.

On a family or sub-department page you can add the whole group at once: press <b>Add category to channel</b> and, under <b>Add all products in this category to portfolios in channel</b>, click the channel. Every product of that family or sub-department that is for sale is added. When all of them are already in all your channels, the button says <b>This category exists in all channels</b>.

## Way 2: from inside a channel

Use this when you already know the product codes or names.

1. Open <b>Channels</b> in the menu and click your channel. Open <b>My Products</b>.
2. Press <b>Add products</b>. The window <b>Select products to be added to shop</b> opens.
3. Type in the search box. By default it searches <b>Product</b> names and codes. Click <b>Department</b>, <b>Sub-department</b> or <b>Family</b> to search by the name of that group instead.
4. Tick the products you want. <b>Select ... products in this page</b> ticks the whole page.
5. Press <b>Add</b>. The button shows how many products you ticked.

This window only lists products that are for sale and are not in this channel yet.

## Other ways to fill a channel

- <b>From a spreadsheet</b>: on <b>My Products</b>, press the upload button (<b>Import from xlsx file</b>). Download the template, write one product code per row in the <b>sku</b> column and upload it. Codes that we cannot find, or products that are not for sale, are skipped and shown as failed.
- <b>Copy from another channel</b>: on <b>My Products</b>, press <b>⋮</b> and choose a channel under <b>Clone portfolio from channel:</b>. All its products are copied into this channel. This button only shows when another channel already has products.

## Check the products arrived

Open <b>My Products</b> of the channel. The number next to <b>My Products</b> in the menu counts the products in the list. On a platform channel, new products show red (not connected) in the <b>Status</b> column until you create or match them in your store, and a yellow bar says <b>You have ... products not synced yet</b>.

## Next step: put them in your store

Adding a product only puts it in your list. To sell it you must send it to your store. See [Using My Products](/docs/my-products) and the guide for your platform, for example [Managing products on WooCommerce](/docs/managing-products-on-woocommerce) or [Managing products on eBay](/docs/managing-products-on-ebay).

A manual channel has no store to send to. You order the products from a basket. See [Managing products on the manual channel](/docs/managing-products-on-the-manual-channel).

## When something goes wrong

- **The button says "Something went wrong" / "Failed to add to portfolio".** Refresh the page and try again. If it keeps failing, ask us in the chat on our website.
- **I pressed "Add to all channels" but one channel does not have the product.** Only open channels get it. A channel that is closed, that you deleted, or that is not connected to its platform is skipped. Add the product to that channel from inside it with <b>Add products</b>.
- **I cannot find a product in the "Add products" window.** It is already in this channel, or it is not for sale at the moment, or it is discontinued. Check the <b>My Products</b> list first.
- **The product is in My Products but not in my store.** That is normal. Adding does not publish. Use <b>Create new product</b>, or one of the match buttons such as <b>Match with this product</b>, on <b>My Products</b>.
- **The spreadsheet upload says "SKU not found in this shop" or "Product is not for sale".** The code in the <b>sku</b> column must be our product code, exactly as shown on the product page. Fix the row and upload again.

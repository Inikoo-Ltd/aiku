<aside class="tldr">
Connect your store as a sales channel, add our products to it, save a payment card, and let the orders come in. We pay each order from your balance or card, pick and pack it, send it to your buyer with our courier, and send the tracking number back to your store. Each step has its own guide below.
</aside>

## 1. Connect your store

In the left menu click <b>Channels</b>, then <b>Add Sales Channel</b>. Choose where you sell:

- [Shopify](/docs/connecting-shopify)
- [eBay](/docs/connecting-ebay)
- [WooCommerce](/docs/connecting-woocommerce)
- [TikTok Shop](/docs/connecting-tiktok-shop)
- [Wix](/docs/connecting-wix)
- [Allegro](/docs/connecting-allegro), on aw-dropship.eu and aw-dropship.es only
- [Manual/API](/docs/manual-and-api-channel), if you want to place orders yourself or from your own system.

You can connect more than one store. Each connected store appears in the left menu under <b>Channels</b> with its own <b>My Products</b> and <b>Orders</b>. A Manual/API channel also has <b>Baskets</b>, <b>Clients</b> and <b>API</b>.

## 2. Add products

Find products in <b>Catalogue</b> and add them to <b>My Products</b> of your channel. For a connected store, you upload them to your store from there. Only products in <b>My Products</b> can be ordered through that channel: an order for a product that is not there will not come over.

## 3. Set up payment

Save a card in <b>Saved Cards</b>, or keep money in your balance with <b>Top Up</b>. Orders from your store are paid by themselves, first from the balance, then from the card. An order we cannot pay waits and is not sent. See [Paying for your orders](/docs/topping-up-and-paying-with-balance).

## 4. Follow your orders

Open your channel and click <b>Orders</b> to see each order and its status. See [Reviewing your orders](/docs/reviewing-orders).

When an order is dispatched, the tracking number is on the order and is sent back to your store. See [Finding the tracking number of an order](/docs/tracking-numbers).

Your invoices are in <b>Invoices</b>. See [Your invoices](/docs/invoices).

## 5. Know the limits

- Some countries cannot be delivered to from each website. See [Countries we cannot deliver to](/docs/delivery-restrictions).
- Marketplaces may ask you for safety documents. See [Product safety documents](/docs/product-safety-documents).

## Need help?

Ask us in the chat on our website, with your order reference or the product code. It makes your question much faster to answer.

<aside class="tldr">
Press <b>Register</b> on the website, fill in the form and tick <b>I agree with the terms and conditions</b>. Your account is ready at once: there is no approval step, and you are logged in as soon as you press <b>Register</b>. You can also register with your Google account.
</aside>

## Start the registration

Open the website and press <b>Register</b>. You see two choices:

- <b>Register</b>: create an account with your email and a password.
- <b>Register with Google</b>: use your Google account. Your email comes from Google and you do not need a password for our website.

If you already have an account, press <b>Login here</b> instead.

## Fill in the form

The page is called <b>Register</b> and says <b>Fill the form to complete your registration</b>. Fields with a red star are required.

- <b>Email</b> (required): this is also your username.
- <b>Password</b> and <b>Retype Password</b> (required): at least 8 characters, and both must be the same.
- <b>Name</b> (required): your name.
- <b>Phone Number</b> (required): choose your country code and type the number.
- <b>Business Name</b>: the name of your business, if you have one.
- <b>Website</b>: the address of your online shop, if you have one.
- <b>Country</b> (required) and your address. Some countries need more address fields, for example a postcode. The form tells you which ones are missing.
- <b>Tax number</b>: your VAT or tax number, if your business has one. You can add it later.
- Some shops ask a few short questions, for example how you plan to sell our products. These are optional.

Then choose your newsletters:

- The newsletter box is ticked for you. Untick it if you do not want our offers by email.
- The WhatsApp box is not ticked. Tick it if you want our offers on WhatsApp.

Tick <b>I agree with the terms and conditions</b> and press <b>Register</b>.

## After you register

- Your account is active straight away and you are already logged in.
- We send you a welcome email.
- Next time, log in with your email (or username) and password, or with <b>Login with Google</b> if you registered with Google.

To change your details later, log in with the account you registered, go to <b>Manage Account</b> in the left menu, then <b>Account Settings</b>. There you can change your contact name, company, email, phone, billing and delivery address and tax number.

To let other people use the same account, see [Managing users](/docs/managing-users).

## When something goes wrong

- <b>This email is already registered.</b> There is already an account with this email on this website. Log in instead, or press <b>Forgot password?</b> on the login page.
- <b>Phone number is required</b>: fill in the phone number with your country code.
- A field is marked as missing under the address: some countries need the postcode or other address fields. Fill them in and press <b>Register</b> again.
- <b>Password does not match</b>: type the same password in <b>Password</b> and <b>Retype Password</b>.
- <b>HTML tags are not allowed.</b> or <b>Script tags are not allowed.</b>: remove characters like &lt; and &gt; from the fields.
- The form shakes at the terms and conditions line: tick <b>I agree with the terms and conditions</b>.
- <b>Failed to login with Google. Please contact administrator.</b>: try again, or register with email and password instead.
- The page says <b>We still prepare your account</b>: wait a moment and open the page again.

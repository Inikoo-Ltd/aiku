<aside class="tldr">
On <b>My Products</b> you can remove a product from the channel, or only <b>unlink</b> it from the listing in your store. Removing one product with its own button also removes or ends the listing in your store on WooCommerce, Wix and eBay. On Shopify, TikTok Shop and Allegro the listing stays in your store and you delete it there. Removing never deletes the product from our catalogue: you can add it again later.
</aside>

## Remove or unlink: which one?

- **Remove**: the product leaves <b>My Products</b> of this channel. We stop sending its stock.
- **Unlink**: the product stays in <b>My Products</b>, but it is no longer linked to a listing in your store. We stop sending its stock to that listing. Use this when you linked it to the wrong listing, or you want to create it again.

## Remove one product

1. Open <b>Channels</b> in the menu, click your channel and open <b>My Products</b>.
2. Find the product. Use the search box or the <b>Discontinued</b> filter.
3. On WooCommerce, eBay, TikTok Shop, Wix and Allegro, press the red skull button at the end of the row. Its tip says <b>Unselect product. This will remove the product from ...</b>.
4. A window asks <b>Remove ... from ...?</b> and says <b>The product will be unselected from this channel.</b> Confirm.

What happens to the listing in your store:

| Platform | Listing in your store |
|---|---|
| WooCommerce | The product is deleted from your store. |
| Wix | The product is deleted from your store. |
| eBay | The listing is ended. |
| Shopify | The product stays in Shopify. Delete it there if you do not want it. |
| TikTok Shop, Allegro | The listing stays. Delete it in your seller account. |

### On Shopify

On Shopify the button at the end of the row depends on the product:

- A linked product shows the unlink button, <b>Unlink product from Shopify</b>. It only breaks the link. The product stays in <b>My Products</b> and in Shopify.
- A product that is not linked shows the skull button, <b>Remove product</b>. It removes the product from <b>My Products</b> straight away, without asking.

So to remove a linked Shopify product: first unlink it, then press <b>Remove product</b>.

### On a manual channel

On a manual channel press the red cross, <b>Remove product from list</b>. There is no store to change.

## Remove or unlink many products

This works on platform channels, not on a manual channel.

1. Tick the products in the list.
2. Press one of the buttons that appear above the list:
   - <b>Unlink (...)</b>: breaks the link with your store. The products stay in <b>My Products</b> and the listings stay in your store.
   - <b>Unlink & Delete (...)</b>: breaks the link and removes the products from <b>My Products</b>. The listings stay in your store, so delete them there if you no longer want them.

There is no confirmation window for these two buttons. Check what you ticked before you press.

## Remove a bundle

Bundles are on the <b>My Bundles</b> tab. See [Creating bundles](/docs/bundles).

## Discontinued and not-for-sale products

When we stop selling a product, its row shows a crossed-out box: <b>This product line has been discontinued. Please remove this item</b>. Remove it from the channel and from your store, so your customers cannot order it.

A crossed-out dollar means <b>This product line is currently not for sale</b>. We send zero stock for it. It may come back on sale, so you can keep it in your list.

## When something goes wrong

- **I removed the product but it is still in my store.** On Shopify, TikTok Shop and Allegro, and after <b>Unlink</b> or <b>Unlink & Delete</b> on any platform, the listing stays in your store. Delete it in your store.
- **I removed the product by mistake.** Add it again with <b>Add products</b>, then use <b>Match with this product</b> (or <b>Match it with an existing product in your shop</b>) to link it to the listing that is still in your store. On WooCommerce and Wix the listing was deleted, so use <b>Create new product</b>. See [Using My Products](/docs/my-products).
- **"Not allowed to revise an ended item" on eBay.** The listing was ended, by you, by eBay or by removing the product. Unlink the product and create it again.
- **My eBay items all ended suddenly.** Removing products from the channel ends their eBay listings. Also check your eBay account: eBay ends listings when an account is restricted or over its selling limits. Look at the <b>Logs</b> tab for the answer eBay gave.

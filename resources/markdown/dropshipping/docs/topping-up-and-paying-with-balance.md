<aside class="tldr">
Go to <b>Top Up</b> in the left menu, press <b>Top Up Balance</b>, choose an amount and press <b>Submit</b>, then pay. Your balance is always used first, for orders you place yourself and for orders that come in from your stores. If an order from your store shows <b>Unpaid</b>, top up enough and press <b>Pay … with balance</b> on the order: it then goes to our warehouse.
</aside>

## Why keep a balance

Orders from your connected stores arrive when you are not watching. We pay them from your balance first, then from your saved cards. If neither works, the order waits as <b>Unpaid</b> and is not sent until you pay it. We do not try again by ourselves. A balance that covers your usual orders keeps them moving without delay.

## Top up your balance

1. In the left menu, press <b>Top Up</b>. The <b>Top Up Dashboard</b> shows your <b>Current balance</b> and how many top ups you made.
2. Press <b>Top Up Balance</b>.
3. Choose one of the amounts, or press <b>Custom amount</b> and type the amount in <b>Amount to deposit</b>. The minimum is 1 in your shop's currency.
4. Press <b>Submit</b>. The payment page opens.
5. Under <b>Online payments</b>, choose how to pay and follow the steps. The methods depend on your country, currency and device. See [Payment cards and options](/docs/payment-cards-and-options).
6. After you pay, you see <b>Payment done. Waiting for confirmation...</b>. When the payment is confirmed you are taken back to the dashboard, with a link <b>Click Here to Download Receipt</b>.

## See your top ups and receipts

On the <b>Top Up</b> page, press <b>View Top ups</b> at the top (or the number of previous top ups). The list shows each top up with its <b>Reference</b>, <b>Amount</b> and <b>Status</b>.

- Download the receipt of one top up from its row.
- Press <b>Download All Receipt</b> to download them all.

## Pay an order you create with balance

When you go to the checkout, your balance is used first.

- If the balance covers the whole order, press <b>Place order</b>. The order is paid and sent to our warehouse.
- If it covers only part, the checkout shows how much is paid with balance. Pay the rest under <b>Online payments</b>.

## Pay an unpaid order from your store

An order from your store is <b>Unpaid</b> when your balance was too low and no saved card could pay it. We also send you an email that the order is on hold.

1. Open the order. It has a red <b>Unpaid</b> label and a message asking you to add funds to your balance.
2. Top up at least the amount the order still needs (see above).
3. Go back to the order. A yellow box says <b>Order … is not paid yet</b> and shows <b>Your balance</b>.
4. Press <b>Pay … with balance</b>.

The money is taken from your balance, the label changes to <b>Paid</b>, and the order goes to our warehouse.

The <b>Pay … with balance</b> button only appears when your balance covers everything the order still needs and the order is still <b>Submitted</b> or <b>Picking</b> (not yet packed). If you do not see it, top up more.

## Money that comes back to your balance

Some money can come back to your balance: when an order is cancelled, what you paid for it; or when an item in your order could not be sent. You can use it for your next orders.

## When something goes wrong

- <b>Payment still processing</b> / <b>Your balance will be topped up automatically once the payment is confirmed.</b>: your bank has not confirmed yet. Do not pay again. Open <b>Top Up</b> again in a few minutes. If your balance still has not changed after a while, ask us in the chat on our website and give the top up reference from <b>View Top ups</b>.
- <b>Something went wrong</b> / <b>Failed to communicate with the payment service.</b>: the payment did not start. Try again, or choose another payment method.
- <b>Failed to Top Up</b>: the top up did not start. Try again, or ask us in the chat on our website.
- <b>Customer has no balance</b>: your balance is empty or too low. Top up first.
- <b>Order has been paid</b>: the order is already paid. Nothing more to do.
- <b>Failed to pay order with customer balance</b>: the payment from balance did not go through. Refresh the order page and try again.
- Your order is <b>Unpaid</b> even though you have a saved card: the card was refused or has expired. Check it in <b>Saved Cards</b>, then top up and press <b>Pay … with balance</b>.
- There is no <b>Pay … with balance</b> button on an unpaid order: your balance is lower than what the order needs, the order is already packed, or the delivery or billing address is in a country we cannot serve (see [Countries we cannot deliver to](/docs/delivery-restrictions)). Top up more, change the address, or ask us in the chat on our website.

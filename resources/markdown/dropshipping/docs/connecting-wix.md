<aside class="tldr">
Go to <b>Channels</b>, press <b>Add Sales Channel</b>, then <b>Connect</b> on the Wix card. Wix opens in a new tab: choose your site and install our app. When the install finishes, Wix sends you back to your new channel in your dropshipping account. Your Wix site must have <b>Wix Stores</b> installed, or the channel stays not ready.
</aside>

## Before you start

- You need a Wix site with <b>Wix Stores</b> installed. We add products to Wix Stores and read your orders from it. You can install our app on a site without Wix Stores, but the channel stays not ready until you add it.
- Log in to Wix in the same browser, with the account that owns the site.
- Wix is available on all our dropshipping websites. Connect it from the website where you have your dropshipping account.

## Connect your Wix site

1. Open <b>Channels</b> in the menu. The <b>Sales Channels</b> page lists the channels you already have.
2. Press <b>Add Sales Channel</b>. The <b>Create Channels</b> page opens.
3. Find the <b>Wix</b> card and press <b>Connect</b>. Wix opens in a new tab.
4. If Wix asks, log in. Choose the site you want to connect and install the app. Wix shows the permissions the app needs; accept them to go on.
5. When the install is finished, Wix sends you back to your dropshipping account and opens the dashboard of your new channel.

<!-- screenshot: the Wix page where you choose the site and install the app -->

Finish the install within two hours of pressing <b>Connect</b>. After that the link expires and you must press <b>Connect</b> again.

The channel gets the name of your Wix site. You can change it later in <b>Manage Sales Channel</b>, <b>Store name</b>.

## Check that the channel is ready

On the channel dashboard, next to the channel name, you see three small ticks:

- <b>App installed</b>: we can reach your Wix site.
- <b>Exist in platform</b>: the site exists on Wix.
- <b>Platform status</b>: the site is ready to sell our products.

Hold the mouse over a tick to see its name. Three green ticks mean you are ready to add products. Continue with the guide on uploading products to Wix.

If we cannot reach your Wix site at all, the ticks are not shown. You see a red message instead, as explained below.

<!-- screenshot: the channel dashboard with the Wix logo, the channel name and three green ticks -->

The dashboard also shows boxes for <b>Orders</b> and <b>Products</b>. Press <b>View all</b> on a box to open that list.

## Connect more than one Wix site

Each Wix site is its own channel. To add another site, go back to <b>Channels</b>, press <b>Add Sales Channel</b> and <b>Connect</b> on the Wix card again, then choose the other site in Wix.

## When something goes wrong

<b>The page says: Wix Stores is not installed on this site. Add the Wix Stores app to the site, then reconnect the channel.</b>
Your site has no online store. In Wix, add the Wix Stores app to the site. Then open the channel in your dropshipping account and press <b>Try to reconnect</b>.

<b>The page says: Your channel is not connected yet to the platform.</b>
We could not reach your Wix site. This happens when the app was removed from the site, or the install did not finish. Press <b>Try to reconnect</b>. The Wix installer opens again: install the app on the same site.

<b>The Wix tab shows a short message instead of sending you back</b>
- <b>The Wix installation was not completed</b>: the install was stopped before the end. Press <b>Connect</b> again and finish all the steps in Wix.
- An error page: the link is more than two hours old. Go back to <b>Create Channels</b> and press <b>Connect</b> again.

<b>You want to start again</b>
Under the warning on the channel dashboard, press <b>Delete</b> next to <b>Or delete the channel and try again</b>. Then connect the site again from <b>Create Channels</b>.

If you removed our app from your Wix site on purpose, the channel stops working: we cannot send stock or receive orders. Reinstall the app with <b>Try to reconnect</b> to use the channel again.

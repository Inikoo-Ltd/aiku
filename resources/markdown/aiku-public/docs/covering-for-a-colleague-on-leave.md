<aside class="tldr">
When someone is on leave, HR can choose a colleague to <b>cover</b> for them. For the days of the leave, the cover can do everything the absent person's job positions allow, is added to their open tasks and tickets, and finds everything they left open under <b>Tasks → Covering</b>. Nobody has to remember to take the permissions back: they go when the leave ends.
</aside>

## Choosing a cover

There are two places to choose a cover:

- On <b>Leave Requests</b>, press <b>Record leave</b>, pick the <b>Employee</b>, then a colleague under <b>Covered by</b>.
- On <b>HR → Absence cover</b>, press <b>Assign cover</b> next to the absence, or <b>Change</b> to pick someone else.

Only approved leave can be covered. The cover must be a colleague who is working in the same organisation, and a leave has one cover at a time. If you change the employee in <b>Record leave</b>, <b>Covered by</b> empties, so you choose again for the new person.

## Who to pick

Some names in the <b>Covered by</b> list carry a tag. Hover over a name to see why.

- <b>Highly recommended</b>: they have the same role (job position) as the person on leave.
- <b>Recommended</b>: a different role, but in the same department.
- <b>Not recommended</b>: the person on leave can see group-wide parts of aiku (more than one organisation, or sections such as Goods, Masters, Supply chain or Sysadmin) and this colleague can't.
- No tag: they have nothing in common with the person on leave. You can still pick them.

Highly recommended names come first and Not recommended names come last.

Why "Not recommended"? The cover borrows what comes with the absent person's <b>job positions</b>. Access given to their user account directly, such as group-wide access, isn't lent. A cover who doesn't already have that access can't reach that part of the work.

## What the cover gets

### A notification

As soon as they are chosen, the cover is told: <b>You are covering for Jane Smith</b>, with the dates of the leave and "You have their permissions during this period."

### The absent person's permissions

From the first day of the leave to the last, the cover can do everything the absent person's job positions allow, on top of their own permissions. There is nothing to tick: choosing the cover is enough. How job positions give permissions is in <a href="/docs/changing-what-a-user-can-see-and-do">Changing what a user can see and do</a>.

### Their open tasks and tickets

While the leave runs, the cover is added as a collaborator on the absent person's open tasks and tickets. This includes ones assigned to the absent person during the leave. The cover stays on them after the leave, because they may have worked on them; take them off by hand if they are no longer needed.

### One place to see the work

<b>Tasks → Covering</b> lists everyone you cover and what they left open: <b>Tasks</b>, <b>Tickets</b>, <b>Chats</b>, and <b>Picking and packing</b>. That last one means the delivery notes they were picking or packing; see <a href="/docs/picking-and-packing-a-delivery-note">Picking and packing a delivery note</a>.

The <b>Covering for</b> panel in the right sidebar shows the same people, with how many items are left to handle and whether the cover is <b>now</b> or <b>upcoming</b>. A key icon means you have their permissions.

In <b>HR → Employees</b>, the <b>Covered by</b> column shows who covers each person who is off today.

## The Absence cover page

<b>HR → Absence cover</b> lists every approved absence that is running now or coming up, covered or not. It also lists absences that have ended and had a cover, so you can see who covered whom. The newest absences come first.

Ended absences show <b>Ended</b> and can no longer be changed. <b>End cover</b> stops a cover before the leave is over, and the borrowed permissions go at once.

## When it starts and stops

- **Cover chosen while the leave is already running:** the permissions and the tasks and tickets come at once.
- **Cover chosen before the leave starts:** they come on the first day of the leave.
- **After the last day:** the permissions go.
- **Leave edited or deleted, or cover changed or ended:** the change happens at once.

aiku checks for leaves starting and ending every hour, so a start or an end takes effect within an hour of midnight. That midnight is UTC, not your local time. In Malaysia, for example, a cover gets access around 8 to 9 in the morning on the first day of the leave, and keeps it until around the same time the day after the leave ends.

## Good to know

- A leave has one cover at a time.
- If you end a cover early, that absence won't appear in the list once the leave is over.
- The cover stays on the absent person's tasks and tickets after the leave.

<aside class="tldr">
You put back, damaged or marked as not returned more than the return expected, so only what it expected was recorded. Aiku has asked for a stock check for the extra put back.
</aside>

## What happened

The customer return lists fewer items than you handled, or part of it was already handled by someone else.

## What to do

1. Check the goods against the return.
2. Keep any extra aside and press **Done** once a supervisor has seen it.

## When to ask a supervisor

A supervisor decides what to do with goods the return did not expect.

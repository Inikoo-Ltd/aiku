<aside class="tldr">
You packed more than the line has picked, so only the picked quantity was recorded. Take the extra out of the box again.
</aside>

## What happened

When your pack reached Aiku, the picked quantity was lower than what you packed, for example because a pick was undone meanwhile.

## What to do

1. Take the extra goods out of the box (the conflict says how many).
2. Press **Done**.

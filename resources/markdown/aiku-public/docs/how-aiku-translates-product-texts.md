## Where machine translations come from

A product or family made from the master arrives with its name, description title, description and extra description translated into your shop's language. Family texts are translated again when the master changes them, if your shop follows the master for families. A product text you have already saved yourself is never overwritten by a translation.

On the edit page of a product or family, each of these fields shows the master's English text above your shop's text. A person icon next to the text means someone has already reviewed it. The language icon on the right, **Get translation from AI**, asks for a fresh translation of the master text into the box; nothing is saved until you save the form.

## What the translator is told

Before every product text, the translator receives a short brief:

- **It is writing for shops in your market.** Natural wording and the product words local customers use and search for, not word for word.
- **Brand and range names stay in English.** Ancient Witch, Ancient Wisdom, Agnes + Cat, Greenman Rituals, Green Man, Pocket Pod and the other brands in aiku are never translated. If a range name keeps getting translated, ask for it to be added to the list.
- **The rest of the family.** When a product is translated, the translator sees how the other products of the same family are already named in your shop, the ones a person reviewed first, so one family does not end up with three names for the same thing.
- **Recent corrections.** The last corrections webmasters made in your language are shown to the translator as examples.

## Rating and correcting a translation

Under a name or description that nobody has reviewed yet, the edit page shows **How good is this machine translation?** with five stars. Click the stars that fit: five if you would publish it as it is, one if it is unusable. The rating is saved straight away.

Then fix the text and save as usual. aiku keeps the English text, the machine's version, your version and your stars together. Recent corrections are shown to the translator as examples, so the same mistake should stop coming back in your language.

Only the first rewrite of a machine text counts as a correction. Once you have saved a text it is yours, the person icon appears, and later edits to it are not treated as fixing the machine.

## What it does not fix

The translator is still a machine. It can pick the wrong sense of a word, for example "charcoal" the colour instead of charcoal for incense, and it knows nothing about your customers that is not written down. Read new texts before they matter, and rate them: the stars are how we see which languages and which kinds of text need more work.

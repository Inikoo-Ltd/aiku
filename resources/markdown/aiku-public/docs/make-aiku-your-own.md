Everything personal lives behind your avatar in the top-right corner. One click opens your profile; from there, <strong>Edit profile</strong> covers who you are and how you sign in, and <strong>Preferences</strong> covers how aiku looks and behaves for you. Nothing here affects anyone else - change whatever you like.
</aside>

## Finding your profile

Click your **avatar in the top-right corner** of any page. Your profile slides in over whatever you were doing, so you never lose your place - close it and you are right back where you were.

The profile page itself is a small dashboard about *you*: tabs for your **To do** list, your **Notifications**, your **Timesheets** and **Clocking**, your **KPIs**, and a **History** of your recent changes. Along the top sit three buttons: **Edit profile**, **Preferences**, and **Logout**.

## Edit profile: who you are

**Edit profile** is the "this is me" page:

- **Photo** - upload a picture and crop it square. This becomes the avatar your colleagues see in the corner, in chat, and anywhere your name appears. A real face beats a grey circle.
- **About** - a short line about yourself, up to around fifty characters.
- **Chat nickname** - the short name shown in staff chat. If your username is long or formal, set something people actually call you.
- **Email** and **password** - change either here whenever you need to. Pick a password of at least eight characters that you use nowhere else.

## Locking the door properly

The same Edit profile page holds two extra layers of security. Both are optional; both are worth a minute:

- **Two Factor Authentication** - flip the toggle and scan the QR code with an authenticator app on your phone. From then on, signing in needs your password *and* the six-digit code from your phone, so a stolen password alone gets nobody in.
- **Passkeys** - let your device itself vouch for you, using the fingerprint, face unlock or PIN you already use. Add one per device you sign in from; the page lists them with when each was last used, and you can remove any you no longer recognise or use.

## Preferences: how aiku looks and behaves

The **Preferences** button opens your personal settings:

- **Language** - aiku speaks several languages; pick the one you think in and the whole interface follows.
- **Theme color** - a row of ready-made colour schemes shown as little previews. Click one and the app changes instantly, for you only. Working in two organisations in two browser tabs? Give each a different theme and you will never confirm something in the wrong one.
- **Chat panel color** - the staff chat panel has its own colour choice, separate from the main theme.
- **Hide logo** - a toggle to tuck the logo away if you prefer a quieter screen.
- **Preferred printer** - if your team prints labels or documents through aiku, choose the printer nearest your desk and print jobs go there without asking every time.
- **Your timezone** - every date and time across aiku is shown in this timezone. It defaults to your organisation's, so you only need to touch it if you work from somewhere else - and then it is the difference between "the order came in at 9am" meaning your morning or someone else's.

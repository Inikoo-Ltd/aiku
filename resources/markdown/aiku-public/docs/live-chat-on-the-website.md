<aside class="tldr">
During working hours, a message in the bubble lands straight in your <b>Customer Inbox</b> like any other conversation. Outside them, the widget takes a name, an email and a message instead of putting the customer through to nobody. That captured message stays a website conversation unless the shop has turned on <b>Answer offline messages by email</b>, which sends it into the email channel so it can be answered from a mailbox instead of a widget the customer already closed.
</aside>

## During working hours

The bubble opens a live conversation, the same as any other in <b>Customer Inbox</b>: it joins <b>Waiting</b> until somebody takes it, and answering it works like the rest of Chat - see <a href="/docs/customer-chat">Talking to customers in Chat</a> for taking a conversation, the customer's side panel, tickets and closing.

## Outside working hours

A shop's working hours come from its schedule, checked against public holidays in the UK, Slovakia and Spain. Outside them, the widget tells the visitor when you are back and asks for their email and a message rather than leaving them staring at an empty chat box.

What happens to that message depends on one setting, in the shop's chat settings:

- <b>Off (the default)</b> - the message stays a website conversation. It sits in <b>Waiting</b> until an agent opens the app, and the reply is written back into the same widget conversation. If the visitor already closed the tab, they will not see it unless they reopen the widget themselves.
- <b>On - "Answer offline messages by email"</b> - the capture becomes an <b>email</b> conversation instead, sent from the mailbox the shop already answers on. The visitor's reply comes back into the same thread rather than opening a second conversation. This needs a mailbox connected first - see <a href="/docs/answering-emails-in-chat">Answering emails in Chat</a> - and is off until a shop turns it on, because an answer sent nowhere is worse than one waiting in the widget.

Either way, these conversations carry an <b>Offline message</b> tag in the inbox, so you always know a message started as an out-of-hours capture rather than a live chat - including when you find one sitting in the <b>email</b> channel on purpose.

## Emailing a customer who is not there anymore

Even during working hours, a customer sometimes asks a question and closes the tab before you reply. <b>&#8942;</b> &rarr; <b>Email notification</b>, set to <b>On</b>, also sends what you write to the customer by email, so they see the answer even if the widget is long gone. It only appears in a conversation you are holding, where aiku has an address to write to, and never on a conversation that is already an email.

<aside class="wayfinder"><strong>Where to click in aiku</strong>
<ul>
<li><b>Turn offline messages into email:</b> the shop's <b>Settings &rarr; Chat</b> &rarr; <b>Answer offline messages by email</b>.</li>
<li><b>Email a reply to a customer who left:</b> open the conversation &rarr; <b>&#8942;</b> &rarr; <b>Email notification</b> &rarr; <b>On</b>.</li>
<li><b>Change working hours:</b> the shop's <b>Settings &rarr; Chat</b>.</li>
</ul>
</aside>

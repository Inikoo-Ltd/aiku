<aside class="tldr">
The app is not allowed to use the camera. Turn camera access on for Aiku in the phone settings, or use a barcode scanner device instead.
</aside>

## What happened

Android asks you once whether the app may use the camera. It was refused, or it was switched off later in the phone settings.

## What to do

1. Press **Open settings** and allow the camera for Aiku.
2. Or use a Bluetooth or built-in barcode scanner, which does not need the camera.

## When to ask a supervisor

If the phone is managed by the company and the camera setting is locked.

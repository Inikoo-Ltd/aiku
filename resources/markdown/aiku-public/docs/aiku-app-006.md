What you tried to open is no longer there: it may have been deleted, merged, or moved to another warehouse. Go back and refresh the list.
</aside>

## What happened

Aiku could not find the delivery note, delivery, return, location or SKO the app asked for. It may have been deleted or merged, or it belongs to another warehouse than the one selected in the app.

## What to do

1. Go back and pull down to refresh the list.
2. Check that the right warehouse is selected at the top of the app.
3. Search for the reference again.

## When to ask a supervisor

If the record should exist and you cannot find it in any warehouse.

<aside class="tldr">
What your action was for was deleted or changed before your phone synced, so it was not recorded.
</aside>

## What happened

A delivery note line, location or SKO in a location was removed while you were offline.

## What to do

1. Check the goods you handled.
2. Put them back where they came from and press **I put it back**, otherwise press **Done**.

## When to ask a supervisor

If you cannot tell where the goods belong.

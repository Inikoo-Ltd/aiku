You were signed out, for example after a long time without activity or after your password changed. Sign in again to carry on. Work already saved or waiting on the phone is kept.
</aside>

## What happened

Aiku no longer accepts this phone's session. That happens after a long time without activity, when your password was changed, when you signed out on another device, or when an administrator ended your session.

## What to do

1. Press **Sign in again** and sign in with your username and password (and your two-step code if you use one).
2. Actions waiting on the phone are kept and are sent once you are signed in again.

## When to ask a supervisor

If you cannot sign in, or you are signed out again straight away.

<aside class="tldr">
Aiku is being updated or is very busy. Wait a minute and try again. Nothing is lost: line work is kept on the phone until Aiku answers again.
</aside>

## What happened

Aiku answered that it cannot take requests right now. This is usually a short update of the Aiku server, which takes a minute or two.

## What to do

1. Wait a minute or two and press **Try again**.
2. Line work you do meanwhile is saved on the phone and sent when Aiku is back.

## When to ask a supervisor

If Aiku stays unavailable for more than 10 minutes.

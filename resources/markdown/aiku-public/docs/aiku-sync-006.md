<aside class="tldr">
The stock delivery was already booked in or closed when your put-away reached Aiku, so it was not recorded. Check the shelf, then ask a supervisor how to record the goods.
</aside>

## What happened

Someone finished booking in the delivery, or it changed stage, after you placed goods offline.

## What to do

1. Check the shelf you placed the goods on.
2. Open the conflict and follow its instruction.

## When to ask a supervisor

A supervisor decides whether the goods need a stock adjustment.

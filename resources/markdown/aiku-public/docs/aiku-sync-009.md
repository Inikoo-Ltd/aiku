<aside class="tldr">
The delivery note is in a picking session, and picking sessions only work online. Do this line again when you are back online.
</aside>

## What happened

Picking sessions group several notes and are kept in step by Aiku, so their lines cannot be changed offline.

## What to do

1. Go back online.
2. Do the line again from the picking session.
3. Press **Done** on the conflict.

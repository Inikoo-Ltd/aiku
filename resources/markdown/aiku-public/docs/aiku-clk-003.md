<aside class="tldr">
You do not have a clocking PIN yet. Use another clocking method, and ask HR to create your PIN.
</aside>

## What happened

Your organisation allows clocking with a PIN, but HR has not generated one for your employee record.

## What to do

1. Clock in another way (QR code on the clocking machine or your employee code).
2. Ask HR to generate a PIN for you.

## When to ask a supervisor

HR creates PINs.

<aside class="tldr">
While you were offline, someone else changed the stock you counted. Aiku keeps both so nothing is overwritten by mistake. Count again, or keep your count if you are sure.
</aside>

## What happened

Your count was made against the stock your phone had downloaded. Before it reached Aiku, a pick, move or another count changed the same location, so your number may no longer be right.

## What to do

1. Open the conflict: it shows what you counted next to what Aiku has now.
2. Count the location again and press **Count again**, or
3. A supervisor can press **Keep my count** when your count is right.
4. Press **Discard my count** if the other change is the right one.

## When to ask a supervisor

Only a supervisor can keep a count over someone else's change.

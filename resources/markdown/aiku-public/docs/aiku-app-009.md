<aside class="tldr">
Aiku found nothing with this barcode in the selected warehouse. Check you scanned the right label, that the right warehouse is selected, and try again or type the reference.
</aside>

## What happened

The app looked the code up in Aiku and nothing in this warehouse uses it. The label may be damaged, belong to another warehouse, or be a supplier barcode that is not linked to an SKO in Aiku yet.

## What to do

1. Scan again, holding the scanner steady and close to the label.
2. Check the warehouse selected at the top of the app.
3. Type the reference in the search instead.

## When to ask a supervisor

If a product's barcode is not recognised: it needs to be added to the SKO in Aiku.

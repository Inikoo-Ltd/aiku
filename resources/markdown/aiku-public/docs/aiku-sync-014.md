<aside class="tldr">
Your organisation has switched off what you did (for example marking a line as waiting, or as not picked). Ask a supervisor how to handle the line.
</aside>

## What happened

Some warehouse steps can be switched on or off per organisation in Aiku. This one is off, so the action was refused.

## What to do

1. Ask a supervisor how to handle the line.
2. Press **Done** on the conflict.

## When to ask a supervisor

Always.

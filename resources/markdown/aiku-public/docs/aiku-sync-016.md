<aside class="tldr">
Your rights changed while you were offline, so the action was not recorded. Ask your warehouse manager.
</aside>

## What happened

Your account's permissions for this warehouse were changed before your phone synced.

## What to do

1. Ask your warehouse manager whether you should still do this work.
2. Put any goods back and press **I put it back**, otherwise press **Done**.

## When to ask a supervisor

Always.

<aside class="tldr">
Another picker or packer took over this delivery note while you were offline, so your action was not recorded. Hand the goods to them or put them back.
</aside>

## What happened

The note was assigned to, or taken over by, someone else before your phone synced.

## What to do

1. Check who is handling the note (shown on the conflict).
2. Hand the goods to them, or put them back where they came from.
3. Press **I put it back** or **Done**.

## When to ask a supervisor

If two people need to agree who finishes the note.

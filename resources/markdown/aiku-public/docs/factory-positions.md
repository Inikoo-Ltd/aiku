<aside class="tldr">
For managers and for anyone wondering what their login lets them do. A factory in aiku has four positions, from the person who plans the whole floor down to the person who makes things. Positions are given on the employee record under Human Resources, one tick per factory, and the rights follow automatically. Nobody edits permissions by hand. The daily screens are explained in <a href="/docs/fulfilling-partner-orders">Working the To produce list</a> and <a href="/docs/preparing-mixes">Preparing mixes</a>.
</aside>

## The four positions

From highest to lowest. Each one includes everything below it on the floor screen, but not the planning powers of the one above.

**Floor supervisor.** Runs the factory. Sees To produce with every tab, creates job orders from it, releases them to the floor, receives them into stock, decides who usually makes what, and keeps the artisan roster. One or two people per factory.

**Mix preparer.** Runs the mixes and bases the artisans depend on. Sees To produce and the Mixes tab, creates job orders for mixes, and releases and receives only job orders addressed to themselves. Cannot touch anyone else's job order. Works the floor like an artisan.

**Foreman.** Keeps the operatives in line. Sees everything on the floor and the job orders, can adjust a job order's items and quantities, and can start a task on behalf of someone. Cannot create job orders from To produce, release them, or change who makes what. The person to ask when a task is stuck.

**Operative.** Makes things. Sees their own floor screen: the tasks addressed to them first, then the open pool. Presses START and DONE, records what was made and what was rejected. Nothing else.

## How a position is given

1. **Human Resources → Employees**, open the person, **Edit**, then **Job Positions (permissions)**.
2. Expand the organisation. Find the **Production** row.
3. Tick one position. If the organisation has more than one factory, choose which factories it applies to.
4. Save. The rights, and the factory itself, appear on the person's login straight away. If the Factory section is missing from their sidebar afterwards, ask them to log out and in again.

A person can hold several positions, in the factory and elsewhere. A supervisor who also picks in the warehouse simply has both rows ticked.

## Things worth knowing

- **The floor is the same screen for everyone.** What changes is what appears first and which buttons exist, not the layout.
- **Positions are per factory.** The same person can be floor supervisor in one factory and operative in another.
- **No position, no factory.** Someone with no Production position does not see the Factory section at all, even if they are a supervisor elsewhere.
- **Organisation administrators** have all of this without a factory position.

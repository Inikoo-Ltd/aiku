<aside class="tldr">
On an order line, quantities are counted in whole SKOs by default. When a product comes in a pack of more than one unit, a <b>broken glass</b> button sits next to the quantity. Click it and the line switches to <b>Cut view</b>: the button turns orange, the quantity is now counted in single units and shows as, for example, <b>2/4</b>. Type the number of units you want and save. Click the button again to go back to whole SKOs.
</aside>

## When you need it

- The customer wants only part of a pack, for example 2 bottles of a box of 4.
- The warehouse tells you some units of an SKO are missing or broken, for example 2 of 4, and you want to take off only those, not the whole SKO or the whole product.

## Where the button is

Open the order. In the products list, look at the <b>Quantity</b> column. The broken glass button is on the right of the quantity box.

It only appears on products packed in more than one unit. A product sold as a single unit has nothing to cut, so it has no button.

## Changing the quantity

**Order still in basket or just submitted.** The quantity box is editable straight away.

1. Click the broken glass button. It turns orange and the box now counts units: <b>8/4</b> means 8 units of a 4 pack, which is 2 whole SKOs.
2. Change the number with <b>+</b> and <b>-</b> or type it. <b>6/4</b> is one SKO and a half.
3. It saves by itself.

**Order already in the warehouse.** Press the pencil (<b>Edit Product Order</b>) at the end of the line first. The box opens with the broken glass button next to it; click it, type the number of units and save the change.

The order total follows the new quantity.

## When the warehouse reports missing units

Items the warehouse could not pick go to <b>Waiting for CRM</b>. When you replace one of them with another product, the replace window has the same broken glass switch under <b>Quantity to replace</b>. Turn it on to swap only part of a pack, for example 2 of a 4 pack, instead of the whole SKO.

## Before raising a ticket

If something on a screen looks like it cannot be done, ask your manager or a colleague first, or search these guides. A lot of what looks missing is already there behind a button. Raise a ticket when it is really broken or really missing.

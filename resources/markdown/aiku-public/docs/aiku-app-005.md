<aside class="tldr">
Your account does not have the permission this screen or action needs. Ask your warehouse manager to add it in Aiku. The app only shows what your account may use.
</aside>

## What happened

Aiku checked your rights for this warehouse and refused. Your rights may have changed since the app last loaded them, or this part of Aiku needs a role you do not have.

## What to do

1. Go back and carry on with the work you can do.
2. Ask your warehouse manager to give your account the permission shown on the screen.
3. After the change, pull down to refresh or open the app again: rights are reloaded every time the app opens.

## When to ask a supervisor

Always: only a manager can change your rights.

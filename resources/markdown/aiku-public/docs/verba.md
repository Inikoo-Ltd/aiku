<aside class="tldr">
Verba is where people who work in aiku talk to each other: public and private channels, direct messages and calls. It is separate from the customer chat on your website; customers never see it. Open it from <b>Verba</b> at the bottom of the left menu. This guide is the map; each area has its own short guide, linked below. Coming from Slack? Start with <a href="/docs/verba-from-slack">From Slack to Verba</a>.
</aside>

<h2 id="words">The words we use</h2>

- **Verb**: a message. "Drop us a Verb when the samples land."
- **V·Desk**: a channel for a team or a topic. A public V·Desk can be read by everyone who can see V·Desks.
- **Private V·Desk**: a V·Desk only the people in it see. It shows a lock instead of #.
- **V·D**: a direct message, to one colleague or to a few.

<h2 id="tabs">The tabs</h2>

Click **Verba** in the left menu. The tabs along the top are:

| Tab | What it is | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **News** | Highlights from your chats and channels this week, your recent chats, and buttons to start a Verb, a private V·Desk or a call. | [News](/docs/verba-news) |
| **V·Desks** | Every channel, public and private. | [V·Desks](/docs/verba-v-desks) |
| **V·Ds** | Direct messages and group chats. | [Private V·Desks and V·Ds](/docs/verba-verbations-and-v-ds) |
| **Calls** | Your calls, missed ones included. | [Calls](/docs/verba-calls) |
| **Analytics** | Numbers on messages, calls and people. | This page |
| **Settings** | Which public V·Desks show in which organisation's list. | [Settings](#settings) |

**Calls** shows only when calls are set up on your server. **Analytics** shows only to people with group-wide access. **Settings** shows only to Verba admins.

Hover over any tab to see a short note on what it holds.

<h2 id="filter">The All, Public and Private filter</h2>

At the top of the V·Desks sidebar is a small **All · Public · Private** filter. Pick **Public** to see only public channels, **Private** to see only your private V·Desks, or **All** for everything. Verba remembers your choice. If you open a channel the filter hides, for example from a link, the filter goes back to **All**. Old links that end in /vx/groups still work.

<h2 id="settings">Settings, for Verba admins</h2>

**Verba admin** is a group job position, and group admins have the same rights. If you hold it, Verba has a **Settings** tab that lists every public V·Desk. For each one you can:

- pick the organisations whose staff get it in their V·Desks list (pick none and everyone gets it);
- or switch on **Hidden** to take it out of every list.

This only tidies the lists. Nobody loses access: anyone who could read the channel can still open it from a link or from search, and channels you starred, have open or have unread always stay in your list. Nothing changes in Slack. Private V·Desks never appear in Settings; only their members ever see them.

Public channels of the AW Advantage (Bali) Slack show in Verba only to people who have an account in that Slack, the same people who see them in Slack.

<h2 id="quick-call">The quick call button</h2>

When calls are set up on your server, there is a phone button, **Quick call**, at the top right of every aiku screen, just left of the moon (focus mode). Click it to see the last 3 people you called one to one and the colleagues you starred, with a **Find a colleague** search for anyone else. Each person has two buttons: a speech bubble to message them and a green phone to call them. See [Calls](/docs/verba-calls).

The moon next to it is **Focus mode**: see [Focus and notifications](/docs/verba-focus-and-notifications).

<h2 id="logo">The Verba logo</h2>

The two overlapping balls in the left menu are Verba's logo. In a call they spin, one ball per person, up to four. In a video or screen call they show a small screen with a pulsing dot. When the call ends they fly apart.

<h2 id="shortcuts">Keyboard shortcuts</h2>

- **Ctrl + J** (**⌘ J** on a Mac): jump to a channel or person.
- **Alt + ↑ / ↓** (**⌥ ↑ / ↓**): previous or next channel or direct message.
- **Esc**: mark the open channel as read. **Shift + Esc**: mark everything as read.
- **↑** in an empty message box: edit your last message.
- **Ctrl + /** (**⌘ /**): show all shortcuts.

<h2 id="analytics">Analytics</h2>

If you see the **Analytics** tab, it shows Verbs sent, people writing, typical reply time, calls, time in calls and how much of the talking happens in Verba, by day or by week, with a list of call quality.

<aside class="wayfinder"><strong>Where to click in aiku</strong>
<ul>
<li><b>Open Verba:</b> left menu → <b>Verba</b>.</li>
<li><b>Tabs:</b> <b>News</b>, <b>V·Desks</b>, <b>V·Ds</b>, <b>Calls</b>, <b>Analytics</b>, and <b>Settings</b> for Verba admins.</li>
<li><b>Hide a public V·Desk from lists:</b> <b>Verba → Settings</b> → pick organisations, or <b>Hidden</b>.</li>
<li><b>Call someone fast:</b> phone button at the top right → <b>Find a colleague</b> → green phone.</li>
<li><b>Jump anywhere:</b> <b>Ctrl + J</b> / <b>⌘ J</b>.</li>
</ul>
</aside>

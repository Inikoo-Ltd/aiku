<aside class="tldr">
The delivery note was cancelled while you were offline, so your action was not recorded. Put any goods you picked for it back where they came from.
</aside>

## What happened

Customer service or a supervisor cancelled the order after you started working on it offline.

## What to do

1. Put the goods back where they came from (the conflict screen says where).
2. Press **I put it back**.

## When to ask a supervisor

If you are not sure where the goods go.

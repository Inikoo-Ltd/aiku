<aside class="tldr">
A <b>V·Desk</b> is a public channel for a team or a topic. V·Desks are the company's Slack public channels, shown in Verba and kept in step both ways: what you write in Verba appears in Slack, and what people write in Slack appears in Verba. If the <b>V·Desks</b> tab is empty for you, read <a href="#who-sees">Who sees V·Desks</a>.
</aside>

<h2 id="who-sees">Who sees V·Desks</h2>

You see the public channels once your aiku account is linked to your Slack account by the same email address. If they are not linked, the tab says **No Slack channels to show**. Slack guests never see them.

<h2 id="reading">Reading</h2>

Open **Verba → V·Desks** and click a channel on the left. Click the star next to a channel (**Add to Starred**) to keep it in **Starred** at the top of the list. Archived channels sit under **Archived** at the bottom.

Each channel has two tabs: **Messages** and **Files & links**, which lists everything shared there with a search and a filter for files, media and links.

<h2 id="writing">Writing a Verb</h2>

Type in the box at the bottom (**Message #channel**) and press **Enter** to send. **Shift + Enter** adds a new line. Type **@** to mention someone, **#** to link a channel and **:** to find an emoji. The buttons give you quote, code, **Attach files**, **Emoji** and **Mention someone**.

Writing **@channel** or **@everyone** asks **Notify everyone?** first, because it alerts the whole channel.

**How your Verb shows in Slack.** If you have connected your Slack account, it shows in Slack as you. If not, it is sent under your name through the aiku app, so Slack shows an APP label next to it. To show fully as you, use **Connect your Slack** under the message box, or **Connect** under **Slack** in your **Personal settings**. Sending files needs the connection. If the box says **Read only, from Slack**, connect or reconnect your Slack account to write.

<h2 id="actions">Threads, reactions and pins</h2>

Hover over a message to see its actions:

- **React** with one of the quick emojis, or **Add reaction** for any other.
- **Reply in thread** opens the thread on the right. Tick **Also send to #channel** to post your reply in the channel too.
- **More actions**: **Copy link**, **Pin to channel** / **Unpin from channel**, and on your own messages **Edit message** and **Delete message**.

Deleting asks first: it deletes the message in Slack for everyone and cannot be undone. Edited messages show **(edited)**.

The pin in the channel header opens **Pinned messages**, and **Add a bookmark** adds a link to the channel. They are the same pins and bookmarks as in Slack.

<h2 id="search">Search</h2>

Use the search at the top (**Search V·Desks…**). Filter by **In** (channel), **From** (person), dates or **Has a file**. Words match from their start: "supp" finds "supplier", but the middle of a word or code is not found. **Jump to message** opens the message in its channel. Search covers the public channels only, never private groups or direct messages.

<h2 id="new">New channels</h2>

New public channels are created in Slack, not in Verba. Once created, they appear in **V·Desks** by themselves.

<aside class="wayfinder"><strong>Where to click in aiku</strong>
<ul>
<li><b>Open a channel:</b> <b>Verba → V·Desks</b> → channel on the left.</li>
<li><b>Keep it at the top:</b> star next to the channel → <b>Starred</b>.</li>
<li><b>Thread, pin, edit, delete:</b> hover over a message → <b>Reply in thread</b> / <b>More actions</b>.</li>
<li><b>Search:</b> <b>Search V·Desks…</b> at the top.</li>
<li><b>Show as yourself in Slack:</b> <b>Connect your Slack</b> under the message box, or your avatar → <b>Personal settings</b> → <b>Slack</b> → <b>Connect</b>.</li>
</ul>
</aside>

<aside class="wayfinder"><strong>Permissions you need</strong>
<ul>
<li><b>Read V·Desks:</b> your aiku account linked to a Slack account (not a guest) with the same email address.</li>
<li><b>Edit or delete:</b> your own messages only.</li>
<li><b>Send files, show as yourself:</b> your Slack account connected.</li>
</ul>

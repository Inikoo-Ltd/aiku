<aside class="tldr">
The phone could not talk to Aiku. Check Wi-Fi or mobile data and try again. Picks, packs, moves, counts and put-aways are kept on the phone and sent on their own when the connection comes back.
</aside>

## What happened

The app sent a request to Aiku and got no answer. This happens in a dead spot of the warehouse, when Wi-Fi drops, when mobile data is off, or when the phone is in flight mode.

## What to do

1. Check that Wi-Fi or mobile data is on, and walk to a spot with signal if you can.
2. Press **Try again**.
3. Keep working: line work (picking, packing, moving stock, counting, placing goods) is saved on the phone and synced later. The top of the screen shows how many actions are waiting.
4. Status changes such as finishing a delivery note, dispatching or booking in need a connection, so do those once you are back online.

## When to ask a supervisor

If the whole warehouse loses connection, or the phone has signal but still cannot reach Aiku for more than a few minutes.

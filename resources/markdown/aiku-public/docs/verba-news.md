<aside class="tldr">
<b>News</b> is the first tab of Verba. It tells you what happened this week in the chats and channels you are in, so you don't have to scroll through them all. You only ever see news from conversations you can already read.
</aside>

<h2 id="start">Starting something</h2>

At the top of **News** are buttons:

- **New Verb**: write to a colleague. Pick a name and the V·D opens.
- **New Verbation**: create a private group. See [Verbations and V·Ds](/docs/verba-verbations-and-v-ds).
- **Call** and **Video call**: type a name and the call starts. See [Calls](/docs/verba-calls).
- **New meeting link**: makes a link and copies it for you (the button turns to **Link copied**). Send it to anyone: colleagues go straight in, people outside the company ask to join and someone in the meeting lets them in. **Open the meeting** takes you there.

<h2 id="highlights">Highlights</h2>

**Highlights** shows news from your chats and channels this week, picked by an AI editor: announcements, product news, people news, operations, sales and celebrations. Each card says who wrote it and where. **Important** marks the big ones. **Open thread** takes you to the message itself.

When there are enough kinds of news, **Filter highlights** lets you see one category at a time.

**Celebrations** gathers birthdays, welcomes, farewells, anniversaries and team photos. **Send wishes** opens the conversation so you can add yours.

If nothing has happened yet this week you see **No news this week yet**.

<h2 id="recent">Recent chats</h2>

On the side, **Recent chats** lists the last people you talked to one to one. Each has a phone and a camera button, so a call is one click away.

<h2 id="slack">Bringing your Slack conversations</h2>

When you open the **V·Ds** or **Verbations** tab without picking a chat, a card **Bring your Slack conversations** shows until you have said yes. That is explained in [Verbations and V·Ds](/docs/verba-verbations-and-v-ds#slack).

<aside class="wayfinder"><strong>Where to click in aiku</strong>
<ul>
<li><b>News:</b> left menu → <b>Verba</b> → <b>News</b>.</li>
<li><b>One category only:</b> <b>Filter highlights</b>.</li>
<li><b>Meeting link:</b> <b>News</b> → <b>New meeting link</b> → paste it where you need it.</li>
</ul>
</aside>

<aside class="wayfinder"><strong>Permissions you need</strong>
<ul>
<li><b>News:</b> any aiku login. You see highlights only from conversations you are in.</li>
<li><b>Call, Video call, New meeting link:</b> calls set up on your server.</li>
</ul>
</aside>

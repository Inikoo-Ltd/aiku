<aside class="tldr">
Aiku shows less in this location than you took, so only what Aiku shows was recorded or the action was refused. Aiku has asked for a stock check of the location. Keep the goods in the tote until it is counted.
</aside>

## What happened

When your action reached Aiku, the location held less than your quantity. The shelf and Aiku disagree.

## What to do

1. Keep the goods in the tote.
2. Wait for the stock check of the location (a task is created for the warehouse).
3. Then open the conflict and press **Try again**.

## When to ask a supervisor

A supervisor or stock controller counts the location.

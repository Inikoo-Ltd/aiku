<aside class="tldr">
A <b>master collection</b> lives in a master shop and is copied into a <b>collection</b> in every open shop that hangs off it. Whatever you attach to the master — families, products, other collections — is attached to each shop copy as well, matched by the shop's own version of that family or product. Build the shelf once, and twenty shops get it. If you are a shop user who only needs to see or tweak your own shop's copy, read <a href="/docs/shop-collections">Shop collections</a> instead.
</aside>

## What a master collection is for

A collection is a themed shelf: products pulled together from any family for merchandising, a promotion or a landing page. A master collection is the same shelf defined once at group level. When you create it in a master shop, aiku creates a matching collection in every open shop of that master shop, with the same code, name, description and image, and keeps a link back so each shop copy knows where it came from.

Shops that open later do not receive existing master collections automatically. Ask engineering to spawn them when a new shop opens.

## Creating one

Go to **Masters**, open the master shop, then **Collections** and press the create button. The form asks for:

- **Code**, short and unique inside the master shop
- **Name**
- **Description**, optional
- an image, optional

Save, and the shop copies appear at once. You can also create a master collection from inside a master department or sub-department, which scopes it there.

## Filling it

Open the master collection. Its showcase lists what is inside it and lets you add three kinds of member:

- **Products**, with **Add products to collection**. Each shop copy receives that shop's own product for the same master product. A shop that does not sell it simply gets nothing for that line.
- **Families**. Attaching a family brings in every product of that family, in every shop, and keeps following the family: a product added to the family later appears in the collection too.
- **Other collections**, with **Add collections to collection**, when you want a shelf made of shelves.

Removing a member from the master removes it from every shop copy, including the products a removed family had brought along.

## Families or products: pick one way

Families are convenient but follow the family, not your intention. A family that mixes products from two countries, or two price ranges, drags all of them in. When the collection must be exact, attach products, not families. A collection built for a discount should always be built from products, so nobody gets a discount by accident.

Engineering has a way to fill a master collection with every product that shares a property such as country of origin. Ask for it rather than attaching several hundred products by hand.

## What the shops see

Each shop copy shows a **Go to Master collection** link. A shop can still add its own products on top of what the master sends, which is useful for a local extra, but anything the master removes goes from the shop copy too. Sales and product counts on the master collection roll up from the shop copies.

<aside class="wayfinder"><strong>Where to click in aiku</strong>
<ul>
<li><b>List master collections:</b> <b>Masters</b> → open the master shop → <b>Collections</b>.</li>
<li><b>Create one:</b> same page → create button → <b>Code</b>, <b>Name</b>, <b>Description</b>.</li>
<li><b>Add members:</b> open the master collection → <b>Add products to collection</b> or <b>Add collections to collection</b>; families from the master family's page.</li>
<li><b>See the shop copies:</b> open the master collection → its shop collections list.</li>
</ul>
</aside>

<aside class="permissions"><strong>Permissions you need</strong>
<p>Master shops sit at group level. You need group-level access to masters to see them and edit access to create or change a master collection. Shop users without it can still see their own shop's copy under <b>Catalogue → Collections</b>.</p>
</aside>

<aside class="tldr">
Each shop can connect <b>one Google mailbox</b> - the address customers write to, such as info@ or care@. Once connected, mail arriving there shows up as conversations in <b>Chat</b>, and what an agent answers is sent by email from that address, in the same thread. It takes a minute and is done once per shop. The one thing to get right: sign in with the <b>shop's mailbox</b>, not with your own Google account.
</aside>

## Before you start

- The mailbox has to be a Google one: Gmail or Google Workspace.
- You need the password of that mailbox, or somebody who has it next to you. Whichever account you sign in with is the inbox that gets connected.
- Shop settings is an administrator screen. If you cannot open your shop's <b>Settings</b>, ask somebody who can.

## Connecting it

1. Open your shop &rarr; <b>Settings</b> &rarr; <b>Customer mailbox</b>.
2. Press <b>Connect Google mailbox</b>. Google opens in the same window.
3. Choose the shop's address. If your browser is already signed in to your own Google account, pick <b>Use another account</b> and sign in as the shop's mailbox instead.
4. Google asks whether aiku may read, send and organise mail in that mailbox. Allow it; without that, mail can neither arrive nor be answered.
5. You come back to the shop's settings with <b>Mailbox connected</b> and the address shown. <b>Open chat inbox</b> takes you straight there.

## Checking it works

Send an email to the shop's address from a private account. Within a minute or two it appears under <b>Waiting</b> in <b>Chat &rarr; Customer Inbox</b>, on the email channel. Take it, reply, and check the answer reaches your private mailbox from the shop's address.

## What happens to the mail

- Mail from a customer of the shop opens a conversation on that customer. Mail from an unknown sender opens a guest conversation.
- The rest of the Gmail thread comes in with it, so the agent reads the whole exchange, including what was answered from Gmail itself.
- Replies leave from the shop's address, in the customer's thread, with the agent's signature and attachments. Each agent sets their signature on their own profile.
- Automatic mail - delivery reports, bounces, out-of-office, newsletters - is put aside rather than shown as somebody waiting. Nothing is deleted, in aiku or in Gmail.
- <b>Report spam</b> on an email conversation blocks that sender for this mailbox.
- The mailbox keeps working in Gmail as before. Agree with the team that customers are answered from <b>Chat</b>, so two people do not answer the same mail.

## Disconnecting

Shop &rarr; <b>Settings</b> &rarr; <b>Customer mailbox</b> &rarr; <b>Disconnect</b>. Mail stops arriving in Chat; nothing already received is lost, and the mailbox itself is untouched. To change to a different address, disconnect and connect again with the new one.

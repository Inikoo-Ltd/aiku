<aside class="tldr">
The inbox has three kinds of label. The <b>left rail</b> says where a conversation is: which shop, and which folder. The <b>tabs and capsules</b> above the list say whose it is and how far along it is. The <b>tags on a row</b> say something about that one conversation. The one to watch is <b>Unclaimed</b>: a red number there means a customer has been waiting longer than agreed and nobody has taken them.
</aside>

## The left rail: Shops

Each shop you work is a line in the rail. The coloured numbers beside it:

- <b>Red</b> - customers waiting on that shop.
- <b>Amber</b> - guests waiting: people who wrote without being logged in.
- <b>Blue</b> - conversations active on that shop, being answered by somebody.

Under the shop that is open, a small table shows the same numbers per channel: <b>website</b>, <b>WhatsApp</b> and <b>email</b>. The squares you light are the filter. Light several shops and each one shows its own row, so one channel can be watched across all of them.

## The left rail: Agents

Whoever oversees chat sees the agents down the side: who is there, who is on a phone call, and how many conversations each is holding. Picking an agent shows what they are holding, across every shop.

## The left rail: Folders

- <b>Unclaimed</b> (the bell) - conversations nobody has taken, past the agreed time for their channel: 2 minutes on the website, 30 minutes on WhatsApp, 2 hours on email. A shop can be given its own times in its chat settings. The list covers every shop, every organisation and every channel, and everyone who works chat sees it, supervisors too. A conversation held by an agent who went home counts as not taken and comes back here. It leaves the list the moment somebody takes it. The red number is how many are waiting.
- <b>Spam</b> - conversations marked as spam, by an agent or automatically. The grey number is how much there is to clear out, never anything urgent. <b>Not spam</b> puts one back.
- <b>Ignored</b> - conversations put aside because they are not work: an out-of-office, a bounce, a newsletter reply. Each one says why. <b>Not ignored</b> puts it back in the queue.
- <b>Trash</b> - conversations moved to the bin by hand. Nothing is deleted; <b>Restore</b> brings one back.
- <b>Highlighted</b> (the star) - conversations somebody starred to find again. The star is on the conversation, so colleagues see it too.

## Above the list: whose it is

- <b>My Chats</b> - the conversations you are holding. The number is how many.
- <b>Colleagues' Chats</b> - conversations somebody else is holding, on a shop you also work. An amber number here means a colleague has unread messages; <b>Take Over</b> to answer them.
- <b>Everybody's conversations</b> - what a supervisor sees instead of the two tabs: every conversation on the shops they oversee.
- <b>Selected shops</b> - shown when several shops are lit at once.

## Above the list: how far along it is

- <b>Waiting</b> - nobody has picked it up yet. The oldest is at the top, so the customer who has waited longest is answered first.
- <b>Active</b> - somebody is holding it and answering.
- <b>Closed</b> - closed today. A closed conversation is never deleted and stays on the customer's record.

<b>Customers</b> and <b>Guests</b> split the list between people we know and people who wrote without logging in.

## Tags on a conversation

- <b>Probably</b> and a name - a guest who looks like one of our customers, from the email, order number or phone number they gave. It is a suggestion: <b>Confirm</b> or <b>Not them</b> in the side panel.
- <b>Possible noise</b> - it might be automatic mail, but it was not certain enough to move. It stays in the queue for a person to decide.
- <b>Put aside automatically</b> - moved to Ignored or Spam by the system, with the reason. One click brings it back.
- <b>Priority</b> - <b>Urgent</b>, <b>High</b>, <b>Normal</b> or <b>Low</b>, set from the conversation's menu. Normal unless somebody changes it.
- A number on the right - unread messages in that conversation.

## Your own state

- <b>You are on a phone call</b> - a call is running, so no new conversation is handed to you until it ends.
- <b>On a phone call, taking no new conversations</b> - the same, shown beside a colleague.

## Folders in the shop's Gmail

aiku reads the shop's Gmail inbox and files every mail it has read into a folder of its own:

- <b>aiku/imported</b> - mail from a known customer. It is in the chat inbox.
- <b>aiku/unmatched</b> - mail from somebody we could not match to a customer. It is in the chat inbox as a guest.
- <b>aiku/filtered</b> - mail that is not work: from our own staff and shop addresses, no-reply senders, bounces, delivery reports, and out-of-office replies to mail we never sent. It is not in the chat inbox.
- <b>aiku/spam</b> - mail from senders blocked with <b>Report spam</b>. It is not in the chat inbox.

Once filed, the mail leaves the Gmail inbox and is marked as read, so an empty Gmail inbox is normal, not lost mail. Nothing is deleted: when a mail seems missing, look in <b>aiku/filtered</b> first.

aiku reads only the Gmail inbox. Mail that Gmail puts in its own Spam folder, or that a Gmail filter moves past the inbox, never reaches aiku. Colleagues should write to each other directly, not through a shop mailbox: their mail is filtered.

<aside class="wayfinder"><strong>Where to click in aiku</strong>
<ul>
<li><b>See who is waiting too long:</b> <b>Chat &rarr; Customer Inbox</b> &rarr; <b>Folders</b> &rarr; <b>Unclaimed</b>.</li>
<li><b>Take one:</b> open it &rarr; <b>Assign to me</b>.</li>
<li><b>Put back something wrongly put aside:</b> <b>Ignored</b> or <b>Spam</b> &rarr; open it &rarr; <b>Not ignored</b> or <b>Not spam</b>.</li>
<li><b>Change a shop's unclaimed times:</b> the shop's settings &rarr; <b>Chat</b>.</li>
<li><b>Find a mail that never reached the inbox:</b> the shop's Gmail &rarr; <b>aiku/filtered</b>.</li>
</ul>

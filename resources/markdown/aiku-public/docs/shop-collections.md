<aside class="tldr">
A shop <b>collection</b> is a shelf of products from any family. Products get in two ways: <b>directly</b>, added by name, or <b>indirectly</b>, because their family is in the collection. A collection copied from a master follows the master. Before you use a collection for a discount, check its <b>Products</b> tab and make sure every product there deserves it. Managers building shelves for the whole group should read <a href="/docs/master-collections">Master collections</a> first.
</aside>

## Two ways in

Open a shop, then **Catalogue → Collections**, and open a collection. Its **Products** tab is the list that matters, and every product there arrived one of two ways:

- **Direct.** Someone added the product itself with **Add products to collection**. It stays until someone removes it.
- **Indirect.** Someone added its family with **Add families to collection**. The product is there because of the family, and aiku keeps the two in step: a product moved into that family shows up, one moved out disappears, and removing the family removes every product it brought.

A product can be both at once. Then it stays even if the family is removed, because the direct link remains.

Collections can also contain other collections, with **Add collections to collection**, which is a shelf pointing at other shelves rather than a merge of their products.

## Linked to a master, or the shop's own

If the collection was created from a master collection you see a **Go to Master collection** link on it. Members arrive from the master, matched to this shop's own version of each product or family, so the shop copy can be smaller than the master when this shop does not sell everything. You can add shop-only products on top. Anything the master removes disappears from the shop copy as well.

A collection created in the shop itself has no master and is yours to fill.

## The tabs

- **Details** shows the collection and its counts.
- **Sales** shows what the collection's products sold.
- **Families**, **Products** and **Collections** list the members, with a count in the tab title.
- **History** lists every change.

A collection is **In Process** until it has an active product or family in it, then **Active**. An **Inactive** one is kept but not shown.

## Webpage

A collection has no page on the website until you give it one. On the collection press **Create Webpage**, and once it exists the button reads **To Webpage**. A collection used only for a discount does not need a webpage, and a collection used for a promotion usually does, because that page is where the customer sees the shelf.

## Checking a collection before a discount

Open the **Products** tab and read the list once. Look for products that belong to a family you attached but should not get the discount, and for products you expected that are not there. Fix the list before you create the offer, because the offer applies to whatever the collection holds on the day the basket is priced, not on the day the offer was created.

<aside class="wayfinder"><strong>Where to click in aiku</strong>
<ul>
<li><b>Browse collections:</b> your shop → <b>Catalogue → Collections</b>.</li>
<li><b>Create one:</b> same page → <b>New Collection</b>, or from inside a department or sub-department.</li>
<li><b>Add members:</b> open the collection → <b>Add products to collection</b>, <b>Add families to collection</b>, <b>Add collections to collection</b>.</li>
<li><b>Website page:</b> open the collection → <b>Create Webpage</b>, later <b>To Webpage</b>.</li>
<li><b>Its master:</b> open the collection → <b>Go to Master collection</b>, when it has one.</li>
</ul>
</aside>

<aside class="permissions"><strong>Permissions you need</strong>
<p>View access to Products for the shop to browse collections, edit access to Products for the shop to create or change them. Creating the webpage needs edit access to the shop's website.</p>
</aside>

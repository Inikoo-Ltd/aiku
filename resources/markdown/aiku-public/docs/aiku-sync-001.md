<aside class="tldr">
When your offline pick reached Aiku, the line needed less than you picked, usually because someone else picked part of it meanwhile. Aiku recorded what was still needed. Put the extra back where it came from, then press **I put it back**.
</aside>

## What happened

You picked while offline. By the time the phone synced, the line already had some or all of its quantity picked, so only the rest was recorded for you.

## What to do

1. Read the line on the conflict screen: it says how many to put back and where.
2. Put the extra goods back in that location.
3. Press **I put it back**.

## When to ask a supervisor

If you cannot find where the goods should go.

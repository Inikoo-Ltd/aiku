<aside class="tldr">
The return was closed before your action reached Aiku, so it was not recorded. Aiku has asked customer service to check the return before any refund.
</aside>

## What happened

The return was finished (or cancelled) by someone else while you were offline.

## What to do

1. Keep the goods where they are and open the conflict.
2. Press **Done** once a supervisor or customer service has checked it.

## When to ask a supervisor

Customer service checks the return; a supervisor decides where the goods go.

<aside class="tldr">
If you know Slack, you already know Verba. For a while you can use both: the public channels are the same in both places, so nothing you write there is lost on either side.
</aside>

<h2 id="names">What things are called</h2>

| In Slack | In Verba |
| --- | --- |
| Public channel | **V·Desk** |
| Private channel | **Private V·Desk** |
| Direct message, group DM | **V·D** |
| Message | **Verb** |

<h2 id="both">Using both</h2>

- **Public channels:** the same channels in both. A Verb written in Verba appears in Slack, and a message written in Slack appears in Verba.
- **Private channels and Slack direct messages:** once brought to Verba (see below), new Slack messages keep arriving in Verba. In Verba they are read only for now, so you reply in Slack.
- **Direct messages and group chats started in Verba**, and calls, stay in Verba.

<h2 id="link">Linking your account</h2>

You see the V·Desks when your aiku account and your Slack account use the same email address. If the **V·Desks** tab says **No Slack channels to show**, the two email addresses are not the same.

To have your Verbs show in Slack as you, not through the aiku app, connect your Slack account: your avatar → **Personal settings** → **Slack** → **Connect**.

<h2 id="bring">Bringing your Slack conversations</h2>

Your own Slack direct messages, group chats and private channels come to Verba only if you say yes: on the **V·Ds** or **V·Desks** tab, press **Read and say yes** on **Bring your Slack conversations**. A private channel can also be brought whole for everyone in it: type `/invite @aiku` in it in Slack. Details in [Private V·Desks and V·Ds](/docs/verba-verbations-and-v-ds#slack).

<h2 id="notifications">Moving your notifications</h2>

aiku can send its notifications to Slack, to Verba, or both. In your avatar → **Personal settings** → **Notifications**, tick **Verba** under **Notify me when** for the events you want there. See [Focus mode and notifications](/docs/verba-focus-and-notifications#notifications).

<aside class="wayfinder"><strong>Where to click in aiku</strong>
<ul>
<li><b>Open Verba:</b> left menu → <b>Verba</b>.</li>
<li><b>Show as yourself in Slack:</b> your avatar → <b>Personal settings</b> → <b>Slack</b> → <b>Connect</b>.</li>
<li><b>Bring your conversations:</b> <b>Verba → V·Ds</b> → <b>Read and say yes</b>.</li>
<li><b>Notifications in Verba:</b> your avatar → <b>Personal settings</b> → <b>Notifications</b> → <b>Verba</b>.</li>
</ul>
</aside>

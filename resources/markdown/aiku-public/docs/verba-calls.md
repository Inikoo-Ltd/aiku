<aside class="tldr">
You can call any colleague from Verba: voice, video or sharing your screen. A call with one person starts from their V·D or the quick call button; a call with several people, or with someone outside the company, uses a meeting link. If you do not see any call buttons, calls are not set up on your server.
</aside>

<h2 id="quick-call">The quick call button</h2>

When calls are set up, the phone button **Quick call** is at the top right of every screen, left of the moon. It lists the last 3 people you called one to one and the colleagues in your team (starred in the [messaging bar](/docs/verba-verbations-and-v-ds#bar)). Type in **Find a colleague** for anyone else. Next to each person, the speech bubble opens a message and the green phone calls them.

<h2 id="calling">Calling from a V·D</h2>

Open a direct message with one colleague. At the top are the buttons **Call** (voice), **Video call** and, if your browser can share screens, **Call and share your screen**. The **News** tab also has **Call** and **Video call**: type a name and the call starts.

Your colleague's phone rings for 30 seconds. If nobody answers, the call stops by itself and they see **Missed call** in your chat.

<h2 id="answering">Answering</h2>

An incoming call shows a card: **… is calling you**. Press **Accept**, the camera button to **Answer with camera on**, or **Decline**. If aiku is not the tab you are looking at, the browser shows **Open aiku to answer** and the tab title flashes. With focus mode on, the card still shows, but nothing rings or pops up.

<h2 id="controls">During a call</h2>

The call opens in a small window at the bottom right. **Bigger** / **Smaller** changes its size. The buttons are:

- Microphone: **Mute** / **Unmute**.
- Camera: **Turn camera on** / **Turn camera off**.
- **Share screen** / **Stop sharing**. Click a shared screen to see it full size.
- Settings (**Microphone, camera and speaker**): pick your devices, and under data use choose **Auto**, **Low data** or **Audio only**.
- **Hang up** (or **Leave** in a meeting).

The bars on each picture show the connection: excellent, good, weak or lost.

<h2 id="meetings">Meeting links: groups and guests</h2>

A call with more than one colleague, or with someone outside the company, uses a meeting link:

- **Before the call:** **Verba → News → New meeting link**. The link is copied for you; paste it wherever you invite people. It works until it has not been used for 30 days, or until the person who made it ends it with **End this meeting link for good** on the link's page.
- **During a call:** **Copy meeting link** gives a link for this call only. In a direct call, wait until it is answered.

Colleagues who open the link and are logged in go straight in (**Join** or **Join with camera**). A guest types **Your name** and presses **Ask to join**. Someone in the meeting sees that they want to join and presses **Admit** or **Deny**. **Lock** stops anyone new from knocking; **Remove** takes a guest out.

<h2 id="calls-tab">The Calls tab</h2>

**Verba → Calls** lists every call you were rung in or took part in, newest first: who, **Voice** / **Video** / **Screen**, when, and how long. Missed, unanswered and declined calls are in red. The **All** / **Missed** switch above the list shows only the calls you missed, and the search finds a person or a conversation. **Call back** calls that person again (one-to-one calls only).

<h2 id="recording">Recording a call</h2>

If you see a red **Record** button in the call window, you can record the call for a transcript and a summary. Guests never can.

1. Press **Record** and choose what happens to the audio: delete it once the summary is written, or keep it for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months.
2. Press **Start recording**. Everyone in the call, guests included, sees a red bar saying the call is being recorded.
3. Any colleague in the call can press **Stop**. The recording also ends with the call. Only the audio is recorded, never the video.

Afterwards the **Aiku assistant** sends you a V·D: **Summary of your call**, with a link to the call page. The page shows the **Summary**, **Decisions**, **Action items** and the full **Transcript**. It goes only to the colleagues who were in the call while it was recorded, and only they can open the page.

<aside class="wayfinder"><strong>Where to click in aiku</strong>
<ul>
<li><b>Quick call:</b> phone button at the top right → name → green phone.</li>
<li><b>Call from a chat:</b> <b>Verba → V·Ds</b> → open the chat → <b>Call</b> / <b>Video call</b> / <b>Call and share your screen</b>.</li>
<li><b>Meeting link:</b> <b>Verba → News → New meeting link</b>; in a call, <b>Copy meeting link</b>.</li>
<li><b>Missed calls:</b> <b>Verba → Calls</b> → <b>Missed</b> → <b>Call back</b>.</li>
<li><b>Record:</b> in the call window → <b>Record</b> → <b>Start recording</b>.</li>
</ul>
</aside>

<aside class="wayfinder"><strong>Permissions you need</strong>
<ul>
<li><b>Calls:</b> calls set up on your server; you can call active colleagues of your group.</li>
<li><b>Join a meeting as a guest:</b> only the link and a name, no login.</li>
<li><b>Record:</b> recording switched on for your server; colleagues only.</li>
<li><b>Open a call summary:</b> the colleagues who were in the call while it was recorded.</li>
</ul>
</aside>

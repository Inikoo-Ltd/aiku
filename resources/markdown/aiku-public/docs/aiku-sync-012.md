<aside class="tldr">
Nothing, or less than you placed, was left to place on this item, so only what was left was recorded. Check the shelf.
</aside>

## What happened

The item had already been placed (by you online or by someone else) before your offline put-away reached Aiku.

## What to do

1. Check the shelf and the item's quantities.
2. Press **Done**, or ask a supervisor if the shelf holds more than Aiku shows.

## When to ask a supervisor

If the shelf holds goods Aiku does not show.

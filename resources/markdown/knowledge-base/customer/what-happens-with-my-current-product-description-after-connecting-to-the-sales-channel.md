# What Happens With My Current Product Description After Connecting To The Sales Channel?

## Overview

This article explains what happens to your existing website product descriptions and metadata when you connect your products to any sales channel available on AW Dropship system.

## 1) Will I lose my existing product descriptions and meta data?

No, not when you connect/match existing products.

When AW Connect finds a matching product in your channel such as Woocommerce or Shopify store and you confirm the match, we only connect it for:

- Inventory syncing
- Order syncing

We do not overwrite your existing:

- Product titles
- Product descriptions
- Meta descriptions
- Other custom text/content fields in the channel.

## 2) Does AW Connect change SEO/meta data fields when connecting products?

When we only connect an existing Websites product (via matching), we do not touch your SEO/meta data fields.

Any SEO plugins or custom fields you already use in your website should remain unchanged by the connection process.

## 3) What happens when I connect existing products?

When you connect existing products to the any sales channel:

1. AW Connect looks for matching products in your store (typically using SKU or other identifiers).
2. You review and confirm the suggested matches.
3. Once confirmed, we link those products so that:
   - Stock levels are kept in sync
   - Orders are synced between Website and AW Dropship

During this process, your current website product content remains unchanged. We do not modify your:

- Descriptions
- Long/short description content
- SEO/meta data

## 4) What happens if I click Upload all as new products?

If you choose to upload all as new products instead of matching existing ones, AW Dropship will:

- Create new products in Channel, and
- For those newly created products we will upload:
  - Pictures (images)
  - Texts (titles & descriptions)
  - Prices

Your existing products will remain in the channel with their current descriptions and metadata. However, you may now have:

- Old products (with your original descriptions)
- New products created by AW Connect (with updated text, images, and prices from Dropship)

This can occur if the same product exists under a custom SKU (for example, if you previously modified the SKU), resulting in duplicate items—one from the original setup and one newly created.

To prevent this, we recommend manually uploading or matching products to avoid duplicates.

## 5) What is the safest way to try this without risking my content?

We recommend:

1. Start with just a few products.
2. Use the "match with existing product" feature to connect your AW Item with an existing product on your store.
3. Confirm that the item is synced between AW and your Store (a check icon would show besides the product on AW Dropship).
4. Check in web store that:
   - Product descriptions and meta data are unchanged.
   - Inventory and orders are syncing as expected.

Once you are happy with the result, you can connect more products.

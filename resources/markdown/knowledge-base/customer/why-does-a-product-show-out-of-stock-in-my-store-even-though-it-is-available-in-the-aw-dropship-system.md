# Why Does a Product Show "Out of Stock" in My Store Even Though It Is Available in the AW Dropship System?

This usually happens because the AW Dropship fulfillment location is not yet activated in your Shopify store. Once the fulfillment location is enabled, the stock will sync correctly and the product will appear as available.

## Why Does This Happen?

Even if a product shows available stock in the AW Dropship system, Shopify will not display it as in stock unless the correct fulfillment location is active.

Common reasons include:

- The AW Dropship fulfillment location is not enabled in Shopify
- Stock is not assigned to the correct location
- Shopify is prioritising another inventory location
- The product or variant is not fully synced yet

## How to Fix the Issue?

### Step 1: Activate the AW Dropship Fulfillment Location in Shopify

1. Go to your Shopify Admin Dashboard
2. Navigate to Settings > Locations
3. Find the AW Dropship / AW Fulfillment location
4. Make sure it is enabled and active

### Step 2: Check Product Inventory Settings

1. Go to Products in Shopify
2. Open the affected product
3. Scroll to the Inventory section
4. Confirm that stock is assigned to the AW Dropship location

### Step 3: Refresh the Sync

If the product still shows out of stock:

1. Refresh the product page
2. Re-sync the product from AW Dropship
3. Wait a few minutes for the system to update

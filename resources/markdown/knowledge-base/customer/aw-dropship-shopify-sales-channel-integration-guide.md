# Shopify Sales Channel Integration Guide

Welcome to AW Dropship! We know that many of our customers run their online businesses on Shopify. To support them, we built an integration that lets you run your dropshipping business directly through your Shopify store. This guide shows you, step by step, how to connect your Shopify store to your AW Dropship account.

## Go to Channels

After you register and log in, you will see the AW Dropship dashboard. From the dashboard:

1. Go to Channels
2. Click Add Sales Channel

_(Screenshot on the original page: the dashboard with the Channels menu and the Add Sales Channel button)_

## Find Shopify

After you click Add Sales Channel, a list of available sales channels appears.

1. Find Shopify
2. Click Connect

_(Screenshot on the original page: the list of sales channels with Shopify)_

## Enter Your Shopify Store URL

A pop-up window will ask for your Shopify store URL.

> **Important:** Enter the default Shopify URL you were given when you first created your Shopify store (for example: yourstore.myshopify.com).
>
> - Do not enter a custom domain
> - Do not enter a redirected URL
>
> If you enter any other URL, the connection will fail and your Shopify store will not be able to connect to AW Dropship.

When you have entered the correct URL, click Connect.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the store URL pop-up)_

_(Screenshot on the original page: the connection confirmation message)_

## Connection Processing

After you click Connect, wait a few moments while our system links your Shopify store to AW Dropship. You do not need to do anything during this step.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the connection processing screen)_

## Successful Connection Confirmation

When the connection succeeds:

- You will be redirected to your Shopify admin dashboard
- You will see AW Connect listed under Apps
- A green message will say: "Your platform has been connected."

To go back to AW Dropship, do one of the following:

- Click Open AW Dropship UK
- Switch back to the AW Dropship browser tab you already had open

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Shopify dashboard with the AW Connect app and the confirmation message)_

## Access Your Shopify Channel in AW Dropship

Back in the AW Dropship dashboard:

1. Go to Channels
2. Your Shopify store now appears as an active sales channel
3. Click your Shopify store to open its management area

From here, you can:

- Manage Orders
- Sync and manage Products
- Monitor your dropshipping operations

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Channels dashboard with the active Shopify store)_

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Shopify channel management area)_

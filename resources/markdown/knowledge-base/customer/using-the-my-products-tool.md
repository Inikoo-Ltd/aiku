# Using the My Products Tool

Welcome to AW Dropship!

The **My Products** tool allows you to manage products that you want to sell in your store. You can search for specific products, view product details, and add products directly to your sales channels. On this page, we will guide you through how to use the **My Products** tool effectively.

Here is the tutorial you can follow:

Once you have registered and logged in to your AW Dropship account, you will be taken to the main dashboard.

_(Screenshot on the original page: AW Dropship main dashboard)_

From the top menu or profile area, go to **My Store**.

_(Screenshot on the original page: My Store in the top menu)_

Inside **My Store**, navigate to **My Products** from the left-hand menu.

_(Screenshot on the original page: My Products in the left-hand menu)_

In the **My Products** section, you can search for specific products using the search bar. For example, you can search for products such as _incense_ or _candles_.

_(Screenshot on the original page: My Products search bar with an example search)_

From the search results, click on a product to view its details.

_(Screenshot on the original page: search results list)_

On the product detail page, review the product information such as price, stock availability, and description.

Then, to add the product to your store, click **Add to all channels**.

_(Screenshot on the original page: product detail page with the Add to all channels button)_

Once the product is successfully added, you will see a confirmation message indicating that the product has been added.

_(Screenshot on the original page: "product added" confirmation message)_

Go back to **My Store** and open **My Products** again. You will now see the newly added product listed in your **My Products** section.

_(Screenshot on the original page: My Products list)_

This confirms that the product has been successfully added to your store.

_(Screenshot on the original page: newly added product shown in My Products)_

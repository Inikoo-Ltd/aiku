# Manage Client Manually Guide

## Log in to Your AW Dropship Account

- Open the AW Dropship platform in your browser
- Enter your registered email address and password.
- Click Log In to access the dashboard.

**Outcome:** You should now see the AW Dropship dashboard.

## Access the Clients Section

- From the dashboard, locate the main navigation menu.
- Click on Clients.

**Outcome:** You will be directed to the Clients management page, where all existing clients are listed.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Clients management page)_

## Create a New Customer Client

- On the Clients page, click Create Customer Client.
- This action will open a new form for adding a client manually.

**Outcome:** The "Create Customer Client" form is displayed and ready to be filled in.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Create Customer Client form)_

## Fill in Customer Information

Complete all required customer details from your website carefully. These typically include:

- Customer Name
- Email Address
- Company / Store Name (if applicable)
- Contact Information
- Other required business or account details

**Important Notes:**

- Ensure all information is accurate to avoid setup or integration issues later.
- Double-check spelling, especially for email addresses and store-related data.

**Outcome:** All mandatory customer fields are completed correctly.

_(Screenshot on the original page: a completed customer information form)_

## Submit and Complete the Setup

- Review the entered information.
- Click Save

**Outcome:**

- The client is successfully created.
- The new client now appears in the Clients list.
- Manual client setup is complete.

_(Screenshot on the original page: confirmation that the client was created)_

After successful creation, the client can now be managed from the Clients dashboard. You may proceed with further actions.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Clients dashboard with the new client listed)_

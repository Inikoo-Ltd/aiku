# Sourcing Products

Welcome to AW Dropship!

Sourcing products is the first step to preparing items for sale on your connected sales channels. Through AW Dropship, you can search, source, and manage products before deciding how and where they will be listed.

When a product is sourced, it is added to your **My Products** list. This allows you to review product details in one place and manage how each product is connected to your store.

This guide explains how to source products and manage them through **My Products**, from selecting items to connecting them to your store.

## What Is Product Sourcing?

Product sourcing allows you to:

- Choose which products you want to sell
- Add selected items to your **My Products** list
- Review product information before listing
- Control how products are connected to your store
- Prepare products without immediately publishing them

## Sourcing Products

### 1) Register and Log In

Once you have registered and logged in to your **AW Dropship** account, you will be taken to the main dashboard.

### 2) Go to My Store

From the top menu or profile area, click **My Store** to access your store and product management tools.

_(Screenshot on the original page: dashboard with the My Store option highlighted)_

### 3) Select a Sales Channel

From the left-hand menu, select the sales channel you want to work with.

Make sure the correct channel is selected before sourcing products so they are connected properly.

_(Screenshot on the original page: left-hand menu listing sales channels)_

### 4) Search for Products

Use the search bar to find products you would like to source. You can enter keywords (for example, a product name) or browse through available categories.

Filters and sorting options can be used to narrow down your selection.

_(Screenshot on the original page: product search with filters and results)_

### 5) Review Product Information

Click on a product from the search results to open the product detail page.

On this page, you can review:

- Product name and SKU
- Price and recommended retail price (RRP)
- Stock availability
- Product description and specifications

Reviewing this information helps you decide whether the product is suitable for your store before sourcing it.

_(Screenshot on the original page: product detail page with name, price, stock and specifications)_

### 6) Source the Product

To source the product:

- Click **Add to all channels** to source it across your store
- Or click the **three dots (⋮)** to source the product to a specific channel only

Once completed, the product will be added to your **My Products** list.

### 7) Open My Products

After sourcing the product, go to: **My Store → My Products**

You will now see the newly sourced product listed in **My Products**, confirming that the product has been successfully added.

_(Screenshot on the original page: My Products list showing the sourced item)_

### 8) Create a Product in Your Store

In the **My Products** list, locate the product you have just sourced.

Click **Create new product** to create a new product listing in your store based on the sourced item.

_(Screenshot on the original page: My Products with the Create new product button)_

### 9) Check Product Status

After the product is successfully created and connected, the **Status** column will update to reflect the connection.

This indicates that the product is now linked and managed through your store.

_(Screenshot on the original page: My Products list with the updated Status column)_

### 10) Unlink a Product (Optional)

If you want to disconnect a product from your store:

- Click the **Unlink product (❌)** icon next to the product

This will remove the connection without deleting the product from your **My Products** list.

_(Screenshot on the original page: My Products with the Unlink product icon highlighted)_

# Export Tool Introduction

Welcome to AW Dropship!

The CSV Export Tool allows you to download your product data once you have products added to your portfolio. This feature helps you manage product information and use it for uploads, integrations, or external platforms.

Here is the tutorial you can follow:

Once you have products in your portfolio, log in to your dashboard and go to My Products. On this page, you will see all products that have been added to your account.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the My Products page listing the products added to the account)_

In the top right corner of the My Products page, click the Download CSV button. The system will automatically generate your CSV file and start the download.

_(Screenshot on the original page: location of the Download CSV button in the top right corner)_

After the download is complete, open the CSV file using spreadsheet software such as Excel or Google Sheets. You will see detailed product information including product status, product code, family, barcode, pricing, unit details, weight, dimensions, materials or ingredients, webpage descriptions, and country of origin.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the CSV file opened in a spreadsheet, showing the product data columns)_

Each row in the CSV file represents a single product, and each column represents a specific product attribute. You can use this data to review your portfolio, prepare product listings, or upload product information to other platforms.

If you have any questions or experience any issues while using the CSV Export Tool, please check the related help articles or contact our support team for further assistance.

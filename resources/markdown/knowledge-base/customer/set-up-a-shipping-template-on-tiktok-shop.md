# Set Up a Shipping Template on TikTok Shop

## Step 1: Go to Logistics

Log in to your TikTok Shop dashboard, go to Logistics and select Shipping.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the TikTok Shop dashboard with Logistics > Shipping highlighted)_

## Step 2: Add a new template

On the Shipping page, click Add Template.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Shipping page with the Add Template button)_

## Step 3: Select the shipping method

A pop-up window will appear. Select Shipped by Seller, then click Continue.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the pop-up with the shipping method options)_

## Step 4: Configure your shipping template

On the next page, enter the following details:

**Template Name**

Enter a name that makes the shipping template easy to identify.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Template Name field)_

**Flat Rate**

You can set the shipping fee to match your pricing. You can customise it for each weight range.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the flat rate settings)_

**New Region**

Use this option to create shipping rates for regions outside UK Mainland.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the New Region option)_

## Step 5: Save your settings

When you have finished all the settings, click Submit.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Submit button on the shipping template form)_

## Step 6: Link the template to your warehouse

After you create the template, you will be redirected to a page that lists all of your store's shipping templates. Find the template you just created and, under Linked To, click to connect it to your warehouse.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the shipping templates list with the Linked To option)_

## Step 7: Update the warehouse shipping template

You will then be redirected to the Warehouse / Pick-up Address page.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Warehouse / Pick-up Address page)_

Under Action, select Change Shipping Template.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Action menu with the Change Shipping Template option)_

## Step 8: Select your new template

Choose the shipping template you just created (not the default "Ship by Seller" option), then click Save.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the template selection with the Save button)_

## Step 9: Setup complete

Your shipping template is now set up and ready to use.

## Important Note

"Once you update your warehouse and shipping settings, all products in your TikTok Shop will follow these shipping and warehouse settings." To make changes later, update the template again.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the completed shipping template)_

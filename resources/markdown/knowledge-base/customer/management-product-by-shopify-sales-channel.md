# How to Manage Products in the Shopify Sales Channel

Welcome to AW Dropship!

This guide explains how to manage products using the Shopify sales channel, including how to add, synchronise, unlink, and delete products.

## A. Adding Products

**Follow these steps to add products to your Shopify sales channel:**

1. **Go to Channel and select your Store Name**

   Choose the Shopify store you want to manage.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: Channel menu with the Shopify store name selected)_

2. **Click "View All"**

   This will open the full product management page.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: View All link)_

3. **Click the "Add Product" button**

   A product selection window will appear.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: Add Product button)_

4. **Select the products you want to sell**

   Tick the products you wish to add, then click the **Add** button in the top right corner.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: product selection window with ticked products)_

5. **Check the result**

   If successful, the product will appear in your list.

   At this stage, the product status will show as not connected, which means it has not yet been synced to Shopify.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: product list with "not connected" status)_

## B. Product Synchronization

After adding products, you need to synchronise them so they appear in your Shopify store.

1. **Click "Create New Product"**

   Find the product you added and click the **Create New Product** button to start syncing.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: Create New Product button)_

2. **Confirm connection status**

   If successful, the product status will change to a green checkmark, indicating the product is connected and visible in your Shopify store.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: green checkmark status)_

3. **If you see a red notification**

   A red alert usually means there is already a product in your Shopify store with the same SKU (for example: AATOM-09a).

   - If it's a different product, go to your Shopify dashboard and update the SKU.
   - If it's the same product, click **"Match it with an existing product in your shop."**

   _(Screenshot on the original page: red duplicate SKU alert)_

4. **Match with an existing product**

   Type the SKU into the search bar and click the black confirmation button.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: SKU search bar and black confirmation button)_

5. **Verify the result**

   If successful, the status will turn green, meaning the product is now connected to your store.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: green connected status after matching)_

## C. Unlink Product

If you no longer want a product connected to your Shopify store:

1. **Click the unlink icon**

   Find the product and select the unlink option.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: unlink icon next to a product)_

2. **Check the status**

   The product status will turn red, showing it is no longer connected to your store.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: red disconnected status)_

## D. Delete Product

To permanently remove a product from your list:

1. **Click the skull icon**

   This will delete the product from your AW Dropship product list.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: skull icon for deletion)_

# Adding and Syncing Products to Allegro

Once your Allegro account is connected, you can begin listing products from the catalogue to your store. The process follows two main scenarios:

## Scenario A: Your Portfolio is Empty

If you haven't added any items to your selling list yet, follow these quick steps:

1. Under your Allegro channel menu, click on **My Products**.
2. Click the dark **+ Add Product** button in the centre of the screen (or **+ Add products** in the top right).

_(Screenshot on the original page: empty My Products portfolio with the + Add Product button)_

A pop-up window will appear showing the full catalogue. Select the items you wish to sell by checking them, then click the **+ Add [X]** button in the top right corner.

_(Screenshot on the original page: catalogue pop-up with products checked and the + Add [X] button)_

## Scenario B: Uploading Products to Allegro

Once items are added to your portfolio, they need to be pushed live to Allegro.

1. You will see a yellow notification banner stating: _"You have [X] products not synced yet"_.
2. Review your product list, then click the **Upload all as new product** button on the right side of the banner.

_(Screenshot on the original page: yellow "products not synced yet" banner with the Upload all as new product button)_

A progress window will appear showing **"Uploading Portfolios…"** while the integration pushes the details, descriptions, and stock data directly to your Allegro store.

_(Screenshot on the original page: "Uploading Portfolios…" progress window)_

When the progress circle reaches **100% Uploading Complete!**, the system will sync the items.

> 💡 _Note: The page will normally reload automatically. If it stays on the loading screen for a few moments after hitting 100%, simply refresh your browser page manually._

_(Screenshot on the original page: progress circle at 100% Uploading Complete!)_

Your dashboard will refresh, and you will now see green checkmarks and a green badge next to your products under the **Status** column. This confirms your products have been successfully transmitted to your Allegro account and are ready for the final step!

_(Screenshot on the original page: product list with green checkmarks and green badge in the Status column)_

## Verification & Allegro Review

Once the upload process hits 100%, your products are securely transferred directly to your Allegro account.

1. Log in to your **Allegro Seller Panel** (_Moje Allegro_) and navigate to your active listings/offers database.
2. You will find your newly uploaded dropshipping products.
3. Note that the initial status will show as **szkic** (draft) with a **sprawdzanie** (checking) label underneath.

_(Screenshot on the original page: Allegro Seller Panel offer list showing szkic / sprawdzanie status)_

> ⏳ **What happens next?** Allegro automatically performs background validation checks on new API-submitted listings. You do not need to do anything! Once Allegro approves the parameters, categories, and codes, the status will automatically change from a draft into an active, live offer ready for buyers.

_(Screenshot on the original page: offer shown as active and live in Allegro)_

# Adding AIKU Fulfilment Location on Shopify

## Overview

After you connect your Shopify store to AW Connect, one final setup step lets AW Connect process your orders.

## Setup Steps

1. **Access Shopify Settings:** In your Shopify admin dashboard, go to Settings.

2. **Open Shipping Options:** Click "Shipping and delivery."

   _(Screenshot on the original page: the Shopify admin Settings page with the Shipping and delivery option)_

3. **Configure Rates:** On the Shipping and delivery page, click "Rates for…".

   _(Screenshot on the original page: the Shipping and delivery page with the Rates option highlighted)_

4. **Add Fulfilment Location:** Click "Add locations" to set up the fulfilment location.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: the Add locations button)_

5. **Select AIKU Fulfilment:** In the location selection section, choose "aiku-awd" as the fulfilment location and click "Add."

   _(Screenshot on the original page: the location selection with the aiku-awd option)_

6. **Save Configuration:** Click "Save" to finish the setup.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: the fulfilment location set to aiku-awd)_

## Result

Once this is set up, "every order placed through your Shopify store can be recorded and processed through the AW Connect system."

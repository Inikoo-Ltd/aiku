# How to Connect a WIX Sales Channel to AW Dropship

## Steps

1. Log in to your AW Dropship account, go to Channels, find WIX and select Connect.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: the Channels menu with the WIX Connect option)_

2. You will be redirected to a selection page. Choose the WIX website you want to connect to AW Dropship and click Continue.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: the WIX website selection page)_

3. Wait a few moments while AW Dropship connects to your WIX store.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: the connection in progress)_

4. The WIX Studio dashboard will appear. You can close this page.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: the WIX Studio dashboard)_

5. Go back to your AW Dropship account and open Channels. Your connected WIX store should now appear there.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: the connected WIX store listed in Channels)_

6. Three green check marks mean the connection was successful. Your WIX store is now connected to AW Dropship, and you can start managing Orders and Products through AW Dropship.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: three green check marks confirming a successful connection)_

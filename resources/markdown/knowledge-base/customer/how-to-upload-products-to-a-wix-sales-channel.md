# How to Upload Products to a WIX Sales Channel

1) Go to **Sales Channels** and select **WIX**. Then choose the WIX store you want to manage.

Under **Products**, click **View All**.

_(Screenshot on the original page: Sales Channels menu with WIX store selected and the View All link under Products)_

2) On the next page, click **Add Products**.

_(Screenshot on the original page: WIX product list page with the Add Products button)_

3) You can search for a product using its product code, or scroll through the list to find the product you want to add.

Click on the product, then click **+ Add**.

_(Screenshot on the original page: product selection window with a product chosen and the + Add button)_

4) The product you have added will now appear in your product list. However, you will see that it has not yet been synced or connected to your WIX store.

_(Screenshot on the original page: product list showing the newly added product as not yet synced)_

5) **Option 1: Match with an Existing Product**

If the product has already been added to your WIX store, you can use **Match with Existing Product** to connect it to the existing listing.

_(Screenshot on the original page: Match with Existing Product option)_

6) On the next screen, find and select the corresponding product already listed in your WIX store, then click **Link**.

_(Screenshot on the original page: list of existing WIX products with the Link button)_

7) **Option 2: Create a New Product**

If the product does not already exist in your WIX store, select **Create New Product**.

The product will automatically be added to your WIX store as a new product.

_(Screenshot on the original page: Create New Product option)_

8) Once the product has been successfully connected, its status will show three green check marks. This confirms that the product has been successfully added and connected to your WIX store.

_(Screenshot on the original page: product status with three green check marks)_

9) **Check the Product in WIX**

To confirm that the product has been added successfully:

- Open your WIX Store dashboard.
- Go to **Catalogue**.
- Select **Store Products**, then click **Products**.

You should now see the AW Dropship product you have added.

_(Screenshot on the original page: WIX dashboard Catalogue > Store Products > Products showing the AW Dropship product)_

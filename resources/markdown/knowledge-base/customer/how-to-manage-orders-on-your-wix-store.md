# How to Manage Orders on Your WIX Store

This tutorial explains the correct order flow after a customer places an order and completes payment on your WIX Store.

## 1) Customer Completes Payment

Once the customer has completed checkout and payment on your WIX Store, the order status will change to: **Paid + Unfulfilled**

> **Note:** There is no need to worry about the Unfulfilled status. This is a normal status because the dispatch process will be handled by the AW Dropship admin team.

_(Screenshot on the original page: a WIX order showing the status Paid + Unfulfilled)_

## 2) Wait for the Dispatch Process

Once the payment has been completed, you do not need to dispatch the order manually.

The AW Dropship admin team will process and arrange the dispatch of the order.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the dispatch processing screen)_

## 3) Monitor the Order Status

To monitor the order:

- Log in to AW Dropship.
- Open the Orders dashboard.
- Find the order using the customer's order reference.
- Check the order status to monitor the dispatch progress.

You can monitor the order status in AW Dropship until the dispatch process is complete.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the AW Dropship Orders dashboard)_

# TikTok Shop Sales Channel: Warehouse Setup Guide

This is a follow-up tutorial for the TikTok Shop sales channel. You must complete these steps before you can start dropshipping from AW Dropship through your own TikTok Shop account.

## Step 1: Access your TikTok Shop dashboard

Log in to your TikTok Shop account at https://seller-uk.tiktokshop.com.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the TikTok Shop login screen)_

## Step 2: Go to Warehouses

In the dashboard menu, go to Logistics and click Warehouses.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the dashboard menu with the Logistics option)_

## Step 3: Edit warehouse address

On the Warehouse page, find your warehouse details and click Edit next to Warehouse Address.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Warehouse page with the Edit button)_

## Step 4: Enter the warehouse address

In the Address field, enter the Ancient Wisdom warehouse address details.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Edit warehouse address pop-up)_

> Note: The address itself is shown only in the screenshot on the original page, not as text.

## Step 5: Save changes

When you have entered the correct address details, click Save.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the saved address)_

## Step 6: Setup complete

Your warehouse setup is now complete.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the completed warehouse setup)_

## Step 7: Fulfilment Setting

After you set up the warehouse, the next step is to set up Fulfilment Pickup. This setting lets your chosen courier team collect the parcels for your customers directly from the AW Dropship warehouse. **Without this setting, your orders cannot be processed through the AW Dropship system.**

### Go to the Logistics menu

Open your TikTok Shop dashboard, then select Logistics. In the Logistics menu, click Fulfilment.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Logistics menu with the Fulfilment option)_

### Open Handover Method Settings

On the Fulfilment page, select Handover Method Settings, then click Pickup.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Handover Method Settings)_

### Enable the Pickup method

Make sure you select Pickup (In Use), then click Confirm.

Your TikTok Shop is now ready to process orders through AW Dropship.

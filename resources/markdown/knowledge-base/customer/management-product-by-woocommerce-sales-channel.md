# Management Product by WooCommerce Sales Channel

This guide explains how to manage your products using the WooCommerce sales channel, including how to add, synchronise, and delete products.

## A. Adding Products

**Follow these steps to add products to your WooCommerce store:**

1. **Choose your WooCommerce store**

   Select the store you want to manage from the sales channel list.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: sales channel list with the WooCommerce store selected)_

2. **Click "View All"**

   This will open the full product management page.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: View All link)_

3. **Click the "Add Product" button**

   A product selection window will appear.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: Add Product button)_

4. **Select the products you want to sell**

   Tick the products you want to add, then click the **Add** button.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: product selection window with ticked products and the Add button)_

5. **Check the result**

   If successful, the products will appear in your product list.

   At this stage, the product is added but not yet visible on your WooCommerce website because it has not been synchronised.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: product list showing the added, not yet synchronised product)_

## B. Product Synchronization

**After adding products, you need to synchronise them so they appear on your WooCommerce website.**

1. **Click "Create New Product"**

   Find the product you added and click the **Create New Product** button to start syncing.

   If successful, the product status will show a green checkmark, meaning it is connected to your store.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: Create New Product button and green checkmark status)_

2. **If a notification appears**

   If you see a warning message when clicking Create New Product, it usually means there is already a product in your WooCommerce store with the same SKU (for example: AATOM-09a).

   - If it's a different product, go to your WooCommerce dashboard and change the SKU.
   - If it's the same product, click **"Match it with an existing product in your shop."**

   _(Screenshot on the original page: duplicate SKU warning with the "Match it with an existing product in your shop" option)_

3. **Match the existing product**

   Type the SKU into the search bar and click the black confirmation button.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: SKU search bar and black confirmation button)_

4. **Verify the connection**

   If successful, the product status will turn green, showing that it is now synchronised and visible in your WooCommerce store.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: product with green connected status)_

## C. Delete Product

**If you want to remove a product from your list:**

1. **Click the skull icon**

   Find the product you want to remove and click the skull icon to delete it.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: skull icon next to a product)_

# Top Up and Pay With Balance Guide

## Access Your Account Balance

- Log in to your AW Dropship account.
- From the dashboard, Click Top Up Balance.

You are redirected to the balance top-up page

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Top Up Balance page)_

## Enter Top-Up Details

- Enter the top-up amount you wish to add.
- Review the amount to ensure it is correct.
- Click Submit to proceed to payment.

The system proceeds to the payment method selection.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the payment method selection page)_

### Important Notes:

- Ensure the top-up amount is sufficient to cover your intended order.

## Complete Payment for Top-Up

- Select your payment method (e.g. credit card, google play, paypal).
- Enter the required payment details.
- Complete the authentication or verification step.
- Click Continue.

Your balance is successfully topped up and updated in your account.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the payment confirmation page)_

_(Screenshot on the original page: the updated account balance)_

## Create an Order Using Balance

- Go to Clients.
- Select the client name.
- Click Create Order.
- Add products to the basket and configure delivery options.
- Click Place Order
- Your Order Has been Submitted.

The order is successfully submitted, and the payment is completed using your account balance

_(Screenshot on the original page: the order submission confirmation)_

## Complete Payment for Unpaid Orders

To complete payment for an order marked as "Unpaid," please follow the steps below. Please ensure your account balance is sufficient before attempting payment.

**Step 1:** Locate the unpaid order. Navigate to the Sales Channel section of your dashboard and select the relevant order number marked as "Unpaid."

_(Screenshot on the original page: an order marked Unpaid in the Sales Channel section)_

**Step 2:** Navigate to the Top Up Balance section. On the left-hand dashboard, click Top Up, then select Top Up Balance.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Top Up > Top Up Balance menu)_

**Step 3:** Add deposit funds. Choose one of the preset deposit options or enter a custom amount, then click Submit.

_(Screenshot on the original page: preset and custom deposit amount options)_

**Step 4:** Complete the top up payment. On the Top Up Checkout page, enter your payment information and click Pay to complete the transaction.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Top Up Checkout page)_

**Step 5:** Return to your unpaid order. Once your balance has been updated, go back to the Sales Channel section and select the order reference marked as "Unpaid."

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Sales Channel list with the unpaid order)_

**Step 6:** Pay with balance. Inside the order details page, locate and click the "Pay with Balance" button.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Pay with Balance button on the order details page)_

**Step 7:** Payment complete – order sent to warehouse. Once the payment is completed, the corresponding funds will be deducted from your account balance and applied to the order instantly.

The order status will update automatically, and the order details will be sent directly to our warehouse for fulfilment.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the paid order with its updated status)_

# How to Manage Products via the Manual/API Sales Channel

This guide explains how to manage your products using the Manual sales channel, including how to add and delete products. You will also learn how to make products appear on your website using the API.

## Adding Products

1. **Choose your account**

   Select the account where you want to manage or add products.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: Manual/API account selection)_

2. **Click "View All"**

   This will open the full product management page where you can see all available items.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: product management page)_

3. **Click the "Add Product" button**

   Use this button to open the product selection window.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: Add Product button)_

4. **Select the products you want**

   Browse or search for the product, then click the **Add** button to include it in your list.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: product selection window with search)_

5. **Check the result**

   If the process is successful, the product will appear in your product list as shown below.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: product list with the added product)_

6. **Make products appear on your website (API)**

   To display these products on your website, you need to retrieve the product data using the API.

   Click the **API** tab, then select **See Documentation** to learn how to integrate the data into your site.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: API tab with the See Documentation link)_

## Delete Product

1. **Click the "X" icon**

   Find the product you want to remove, then click the **X** icon next to it to delete it from your list.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: X icon next to a product)_

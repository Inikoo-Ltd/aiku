# WooCommerce Sales Channel Integration Guide

Welcome to AW Dropship! We know that many of our customers run their online businesses on WooCommerce. To support them, we built an integration that lets you run your dropshipping business directly from your WooCommerce store.

## Go to Channels

From your AW Dropship dashboard:

1. Go to Channels
2. Click Add Sales Channel

_(Screenshot on the original page: the dashboard with the Channels menu)_

## Select WooCommerce

1. Find WooCommerce
2. Click Connect

_(Screenshot on the original page: the sales channel options with WooCommerce highlighted)_

## Enter Your Account Details

A pop-up window asks for your store details.

1. Enter your WooCommerce account name
2. Click Next

_(Screenshot on the original page: the account name form)_

## Authentication Settings

1. Enter the URL of your WooCommerce store
2. Click Auth Store

_(Screenshot on the original page: the store URL field and the Auth Store button)_

## Approve the Integration

You will be redirected to WooCommerce.

1. Review the integration request
2. Click Approve

_(Screenshot on the original page: the WooCommerce approval page)_

## Confirm the Connection

1. Click Next

_(Screenshot on the original page: the connection confirmation message)_

## Access Your Store

1. Go back to Channels
2. Your WooCommerce store now appears as an active sales channel
3. Click the store name to start managing it

_(Screenshot on the original page: the list of active sales channels)_

From here, you can manage orders, sync and manage products, and run your dropshipping operations through AW Dropship.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the store management dashboard)_

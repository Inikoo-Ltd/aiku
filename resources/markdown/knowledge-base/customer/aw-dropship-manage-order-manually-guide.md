# Manage Order Manually Guide

## Log in to Your AW Dropship Account

- Open the AW Dropship platform in your browser
- Enter your registered email address and password.
- Click Log In to access the dashboard.

You should now see the AW Dropship dashboard.

## Access the Clients Section

- From the dashboard, locate the main navigation menu.
- Click on Clients.

You will be directed to the Clients management page, where all existing clients are listed.

Or if you don't have the existing clients, you can follow tutorial of adding new customer here: https://aw-dropship.info/knowladge-base/aw-dropship-manage-client-manually-guide/

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Clients management page)_

## Create an Order

- Select the client name from the Clients list.
- You will be redirected to the Client Overview page.
- Click Create Order.
- You will then be taken to the Basket page.

The order creation process is initiated, and the basket page is displayed.

## Add Products to the Basket

- Add the required products to the basket.
- Confirm that the client name displayed is correct.
- Verify the product names and quantities before proceeding.
- Enable the Collection toggle if you plan to collect the order from our store and deliver it to the customer yourself.
- Leave the Collection toggle off if you want AW Dropship to deliver the order directly to the client.
- Select Same Day Dispatch if the order requires priority processing.
- Enable Protective Packing for fragile or breakable items.
- Click Continue to Checkout.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Basket page with product details)_

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Collection toggle)_

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Same Day Dispatch and Protective Packing options)_

### Important Notes:

- Always double-check product quantities and delivery options before proceeding to checkout.
- Dispatch and packing options may affect delivery time and additional costs.
- Ensure the selected client and delivery method match the customer's request.

All products, delivery preferences, and order options are correctly configured.

## Submit the Order and Enter Payment Details

- Enter your credit card details as required.
- Complete the authentication password for payment verification.
- Click Continue to finalize the transaction.

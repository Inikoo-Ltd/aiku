# Account Registration

Welcome to the AW Dropship UK website. This guide shows the steps to register a business account on AW Dropship UK.

## Registration Steps

### Step 1: Access Registration

On the homepage, click the "Register" text in the top menu or select the "Start Dropshipping" button.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the homepage with the Register menu link and the Start Dropshipping button)_

### Step 2: Choose Registration Method

You will see two registration options, or you can register via Google. Click Register.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the registration method options, including the Google alternative)_

### Step 3: Complete Required Information

On the Register page, fill in all the information required for account approval, including:

- Email address
- Name
- Phone number
- Country

_(Screenshot on the original page: the registration form with the required fields)_

### Step 4: Accept Terms and Create Account

Check that the information you entered is correct, then tick the terms and conditions box. Click Register.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the terms and conditions checkbox and Register button)_

### Step 5: Account Activation

Congratulations, your AW Dropship UK account is now active. You can start managing orders and products straight away.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the account activation confirmation screen)_

# Viewing and Paying Invoices

In AW Dropship, invoices are generated after an order has been successfully created and paid. Through the **Invoices** section, you can view invoice details such as product costs, shipping fees, and additional charges.

## Steps to View and Pay Invoices

1. After registering and logging into your AW Dropship account, you will be taken to the main dashboard.
2. From the top menu or profile area, go to **My Store**.
3. Click **Create Order** to start creating a new order.
4. In the _Create Order_ pop-up window, select a **Customer Client** from the list. If the client already exists, simply select it. Otherwise, you can create a new client.
5. After selecting the customer, proceed to add products to the order. Use the product list to choose items and adjust the quantity as needed.
6. Once all products have been added, review the order details and click **Continue to Checkout**.
7. On the checkout page, review the order summary, including item prices and shipping costs. Proceed with the payment by entering your payment details and completing the transaction.
8. After the payment is successfully processed, you will see a confirmation message indicating that the order has been submitted.
9. To view the invoice, go to the **Invoices** section from the left-hand menu.
10. In the **Invoices** list, select the invoice related to your order. You will be able to view detailed information such as:
    - Product costs
    - Shipping fees
    - Additional charges
    - Total amount paid

## Important Note

Invoices will only appear after an order has been completed and payment has been successfully processed.

# Deleting a Product from Your 'My Products' Feature

Welcome to AW Dropship!

In AW Dropship, **My Products** shows all products that you have added to your store. If you no longer wish to sell a product, you can easily remove it from your My Products list. On this page, we will guide you through the steps to delete a product from My Products.

Here is the tutorial you can follow:

Once you have registered and logged in to your AW Dropship account, you will be taken to the main dashboard.

_(Screenshot on the original page: AW Dropship main dashboard)_

From the top menu or profile area, go to **My Store**.

_(Screenshot on the original page: My Store in the top menu)_

From the left-hand menu, click **My Products**.
Here, you will see a list of all products that have been added to your store.

Then find the product you want to remove from the list.

_(Screenshot on the original page: My Products list view)_

Click the **Remove product** (❌ icon) next to the selected product.
You will see a tooltip showing _"Remove product from list"_.

_(Screenshot on the original page: ❌ icon with the "Remove product from list" tooltip)_

Confirm the action if prompted.
Once confirmed, the product will be removed from your **My Products** list.

_(Screenshot on the original page: removal confirmation)_

That's it — the product has been successfully deleted from your My Products.

> **Important Note:** Removing a product from **My Products** only removes it from your store's product list. It does not delete the product from the AW Dropship catalogue.

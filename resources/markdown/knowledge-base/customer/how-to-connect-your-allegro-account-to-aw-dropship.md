# How to Connect Your Allegro Account to AW Dropship

When you connect your Allegro seller account to AW Dropship, you can "seamlessly sync our catalogue, automatically manage inventory levels, and import your orders in real time."

## Connection Process

### Step 1: Open Your Sales Channels

- Log in to your AW Dropship dashboard.
- Go to **Channels** in the left sidebar.
- Click **+ Add Sales Channel** in the top right.

### Step 2: Choose Allegro

- On the "Create Channels" page, find Allegro.
- Click the **Connect** button under the Allegro logo.

### Step 3: Log In and Authorise on Allegro

- You will be redirected to Allegro's official login page.
- Enter your Business Seller login details.
- Click **ZALOGUJ SIĘ** (Log In).

### Step 4: Verify Your Connection Status

- After you log in, go back to your AW Dropship dashboard.
- Your Allegro store appears in your channels list. Green check marks confirm that the connection was successful.

## Important Note for New Sellers

If you are a new Allegro seller, you must set up your store policies before your listings can go live. In your Allegro dashboard, complete at least one template for each of these:

- **Warunki zwrotów** (Return Policy)
- **Warunki reklamacji** (Complaint Policy)

If you are an existing Allegro seller, you can skip this step. Your existing policies apply automatically.

# TikTok Sales Channel Integration Guide

Follow the steps below to connect your TikTok Shop to AW Dropship.

## Step 1: Go to Channels

In your dashboard, go to the Channels section and click Add Sales Channel.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the dashboard with the Channels section and the Add Sales Channel button)_

## Step 2: Select TikTok

Choose TikTok from the available sales channels and click Connect.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the sales channel list with TikTok highlighted)_

## Step 3: Authorise Store

A pop-up window will appear. Click Auth Store to start connecting.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Auth Store pop-up)_

## Step 4: Complete TikTok Shop registration

You will be redirected to the TikTok Shop page.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the TikTok Shop registration page)_

At **Step 01: Select Account Type**:

- Select Europe Seller
- Choose United Kingdom
- Click Next

> **Note:** Currently, TikTok Shop registration is only available for the UK. Registration for Spain and Germany is not available yet. We will post an update once these regions are supported.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the account type selection screen)_

## Step 5: Renew

You will be redirected to **Step 02: Renew**. Click Confirm to Install.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Renew step with the Confirm to Install button)_

## Step 6: Authorise permissions

You will then be redirected to **Step 03: Authorise**.

Read the information shown, tick the "I acknowledge…" box, then click Authorise.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Authorise permissions screen)_

## Step 7: Complete authorisation

When authorisation succeeds, click Done.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the successful authorisation confirmation)_

## Step 8: Return to AW Dropship

After you finish authorising, go back to the Auth Store pop-up in AW Dropship and click Next.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Auth Store pop-up in AW Dropship)_

## Step 9: Select your shop

Choose your shop from the list and click Next.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the shop selection screen)_

## Step 10: Connection successful

A Connected notification will appear. Click OK.

Your TikTok Shop is now connected.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Connected notification)_

## Step 11: Verify your connection

Go back to Channels, find your TikTok Shop name and click Enter.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the Channels section with the connected TikTok Shop)_

## Step 12: Manage your TikTok Shop dashboard

Your connected TikTok Shop dashboard now appears in AW Dropship.

From here, you can view orders and products, so you can manage and sell AW Dropship products directly through your TikTok Shop.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the TikTok Shop dashboard in AW Dropship)_

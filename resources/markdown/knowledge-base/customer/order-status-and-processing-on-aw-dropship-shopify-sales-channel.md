# Order Status and Processing (Shopify Sales Channel)

## 1. Why does my order still show as "Submitted" after a few days?

Orders may remain in the "Submitted" status if payment has not been completed or processed. For example, if you have not saved your card or topped up your credit, the order will not be sent to the warehouse for picking and dispatch.

## 2. What should I do if my order is stuck in "Submitted"?

- **Check Payment**: Ensure your payment method is saved and your account has sufficient credit.
- **Top Up Credit**: If needed, top up your credit so it can be used towards your order.
- **Contact Support**: If you need to pay by card, email customer service to send you a payment link.

## 3. How long does it take for an order to move from "Submitted" to "Dispatched"?

Once payment is confirmed, the order is sent to the warehouse for picking. Please contact us via email if you have paid for your order at a later date so that we can prioritise dispatch.

## 4. How can I track the progress of my order?

You can monitor the status of your order directly on your dashboard. Updates will be reflected there as your order moves through picking and dispatch.

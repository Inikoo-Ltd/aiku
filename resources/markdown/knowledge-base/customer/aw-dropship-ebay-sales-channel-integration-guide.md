# eBay Sales Channel Integration Guide

Welcome to AW Dropship! This tutorial shows you how to connect your eBay account to AW Dropship.

## Log In to Your AW Dropship Account

First, make sure you have registered and logged in to your AW Dropship account. From the dashboard:

1. Go to Channels
2. Click Add Sales Channel

_(Screenshot on the original page: the dashboard with the Channels menu and the Add Sales Channel button)_

## Select eBay as Your Sales Channel

After you click Add Sales Channel, a list of available platforms appears.

1. Find eBay
2. Click Create or Connect

_(Screenshot on the original page: the list of sales channels with eBay highlighted)_

_(Screenshot on the original page: the pop-up window for eBay account details)_

## Enter Your eBay Account Name

1. Enter your eBay Account Name (Seller Account)
2. This is the unique seller ID provided by eBay.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the account name form)_

## Authenticate Your eBay Account

1. Click Auth Store
2. Wait while the system processes the authentication.
3. Once the authentication is successful, click Next to continue.

_(Screenshot on the original page: three views of the authentication settings screen)_

## Configure Business Policies

Make sure you fill in the following fields correctly:

1. Return Business Policy → Select Profile
2. Return Settings → Set to Returns Accepted
3. Shipping → Select Profile
4. Payment Business Policy → Select Profile

_(Screenshot on the original page: the business policies form)_

## Connection Completed

Congratulations! Your eBay account is now connected to the AW Dropship system.

_(Screenshot on the original page: the success confirmation screen)_

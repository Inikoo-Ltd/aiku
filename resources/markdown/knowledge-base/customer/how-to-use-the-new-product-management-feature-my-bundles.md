# How to Use the New Product Management Feature: My Bundles

## 1. What is "My Bundles"?

My Bundles lets you combine multiple products into one bundle that you can sell under your own name.

## 2. What's the use?

Create bundles that stand out from standard listings.

With My Bundles, you can:

- Create gift sets and themed bundles
- Make your listings more engaging and complete
- Turn products into higher-value offers

## 3. What are the advantages?

Get your bundles live in minutes. My Bundles helps you get new offers live faster with:

- **AI-assisted content:** Generate product titles and descriptions quickly
- **Flexible naming:** Create product names that suit your audience
- **Faster setup:** Build new offers in minutes instead of hours
- **Retail-ready appeal:** Create bundles that are ideal for gifting, promotions, and themed selling

## 4. How do I use the My Bundles feature?

Let's take a look at how it works. There are two ways to create a bundle – from the My Bundles page, or while browsing products. We'll start with the main method.

Follow the tutorial step by step to create your first bundle.

1. Log in to your AW Dropship account.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: AW Dropship login / dashboard)_

2. On the dashboard on the left, select **My Products**.

   You will see two options for managing products: **My Products** and **My Bundles**.

   Select **My Bundles** to use this feature.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: left menu with My Products and My Bundles options)_

   Alternatively, click the bundle icon in the top menu to go to My Bundles.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: bundle icon in the top menu)_

3. Click **Create Bundle** to open the bundle builder.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: My Bundles page with the Create Bundle button)_

4. On this screen, you'll set up your bundle.

   On this page, you will see the following options:

   _(Screenshot on the original page: bundle builder showing title, product search and pricing areas)_

   a. **Bundle Title**

   Start with your bundle title – you can enter your own, or use **Generate AI** to create one based on your products.

   b. **Add Products to Your Bundle (Search Feature)**

   Use the search bar to find products by name, category, or department, and add them to your bundle.

   c. **Pricing**

   As you add products, your pricing updates automatically – including cost, RRP, and profit.

5. To create a bundle, select the products you want.

   You can select them manually or use the search feature.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: product selection in the bundle builder)_

   **Example:**

   For example, you can search for a theme like "Bali" and quickly build a themed bundle.

   Once you're happy with your selection, click **Next**.

6. In the next step, you'll need to add a bundle description and product images.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: bundle description and media step)_

   a. **Description**

   You can write a description manually or use the **Generate AI** feature.

   The results from Generate AI can be used immediately or edited as needed.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: AI-generated bundle description)_

   b. **Media (Product Images)**

   You can select images from **Existing Media**, which are images from previously selected products.

   Alternatively, you can use the **Generate AI** feature to create new images.

   To generate an image, select multiple images first, then enter a short description. Currently, you can generate one AI image per bundle.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: media selection with Generate AI image option)_

7. When everything is ready, click **Create Bundle**.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: Create Bundle button on the final step)_

8. Your bundle will now appear in My Bundles, ready to list and sell.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: new bundle listed in My Bundles)_

### Alternate method: creating a bundle while browsing products

Here is an additional explanation for creating bundles using the alternate method:

1. After logging in, navigate to one of the departments.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: department navigation)_

2. In the product listing section, select a product by clicking the **"Create Bundle"** button.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: Create Bundle button on a product in the listing)_

3. You can then add the products you want to include in the bundle. A list of selected products will appear on the right-hand side of the screen.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: selected products list on the right-hand side)_

4. Next, select a sales channel, enter a bundle title, and click **Next**.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: sales channel selection and bundle title field)_

5. Then, write a description and choose a product image to represent the bundle. Once finished, click **Create Bundle**.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: description and image selection with Create Bundle button)_

6. You can access your bundles via the **"Add Bundles"** button in the header, or through your dashboard by selecting **My Products** and then **My Bundles**.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: Add Bundles button in the header)_

# Setting Up Payment Cards and Options

Get ready to start selling by setting up your payment methods. This tutorial explains how to input your card information and choose the payment options that work best for you.

Here is the tutorial you can follow:

1. Once you register, you will see this dashboard on your screen.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: the dashboard shown after registration)_

2. You can go to the floating menu on the side and then click Basket

   _(Screenshot on the original page: the side floating menu with Basket)_

3. Go the corner and click create order

   _(Screenshot on the original page: the create order button in the corner)_

4. Input your client name and create your order

   _(Screenshot on the original page: the client name field when creating an order)_

5. The system will redirect you to the checkout page to add your product.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: the checkout page for adding products)_

6. Proceed to checkout. When the payment options appear, you can choose between Credit Card, Google Pay, or PayPal.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: payment options Credit Card, Google Pay and PayPal)_

7. If you select the card option, a form will appear where you can enter your credit card details.

   _(Screenshot on the original page: the credit card details form)_

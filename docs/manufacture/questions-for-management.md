# Manufacture system — questions for management

We are building the new system that replaces the paper sheets on the production
line. Workers will get a tablet: they see what to make, press START, press DONE,
and type how many they made. Pay is then worked out automatically from that.

To finish it properly we need answers to the questions below. Real examples
(a photo of a filled-in paper sheet, one worker's week and what they got paid)
are worth more than long explanations.

## Pay

1. **The bonus targets.** Each task has a lower target and an upper target (for
   example 100 and 200 on bottle filling). 100 and 200 of *what*? Bottles per
   hour, bottles in one sitting, or bottles in a whole day? We need this to pay
   the bonus automatically.
2. **Rejected units.** If a worker makes 100 and 5 are rejected, do they get
   paid for 100 or for 95? Is it the same rule for every task?
3. **The rates.** We need the current price list: for every task, how much is
   paid per unit. Who keeps this list today, and who is allowed to change it?
4. **Corrections.** When a worker writes a wrong number on paper today, who
   fixes it and how? In the new system we plan that only a supervisor can
   correct a finished entry — is that right?

## How the work runs

5. **Choosing work.** Today, does a worker pick what to do next themselves, or
   does a supervisor tell each person what to work on? The tablet can do
   either.
6. **Order of steps.** When a product needs several steps (mix, fill, label) —
   can labelling start before all the filling is finished, or must each step
   fully finish first?
7. **Working together.** Do several people ever work on the same task of the
   same order at the same time? (The system supports this — each person's
   count is their own — we just want to confirm it happens.)
8. **Breaks.** The tablet records the time from START to DONE. If someone
   starts a task, goes to lunch, and finishes after, the time looks longer
   than the real work. Is time-per-task something you care about for pay, or
   only the quantity made?
9. **Rejects afterwards.** When rejected units are re-worked or thrown away,
   does anyone need to record what happened to them?
10. **Who can make what.** Different workers are specialised in different
    products, but there are hundreds of products. Should the tablet show each
    worker only the tasks they know how to do — and if so, who keeps the list
    of who can do what? Or does everyone see everything and the supervisor
    decides on the floor?

## The production lines

11. **Products and steps.** For each product family: what are the steps to make
    it, in what order, and which of them are paid per unit? (This is the
    "recipe" we must type into the system once per product — we need someone
    from the floor to sit with us and go through the list.)
12. **The paper sheet.** Please give us a photo or copy of the sheet workers
    fill in today, and of the sheet the office types it into. We want the new
    screen to ask for exactly the same things, nothing more.

## Tablets and people

13. **Devices.** How many tablets per line do we need, and is there wifi
    everywhere the workers stand?
14. **Logging in.** Is typing a username and password acceptable for workers,
    or would a card / code to scan be much better on the floor?
15. **Languages.** For the Slovak and Spanish lines: which language must the
    worker screens be in?
16. **First line.** Which line or product family should try the system first,
    running alongside the paper for two weeks so we can compare the numbers?
